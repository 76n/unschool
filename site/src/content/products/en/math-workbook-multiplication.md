Owlie sets off on a journey through Times Table Land, and your child comes along. At every station they
meet a new number through jumps, groups and arrays, practise it in a maze and a multiplication flower,
colour a hidden picture by the answers and review everything learned so far.

The tasks are in Hebrew and take 15 to 20 minutes a day, four or five days a week. The progress chart and the journey
map show how far your child has come, and a final test and a certificate wait at the end. Answers to
every task are at the back, so a parent can check without preparing.
