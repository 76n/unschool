For many children Hebrew is not one more subject but the condition without which the other subjects
stall. So lessons are built around real school situations: the teacher's explanation, a text in the
book, answering in front of the class, a written assignment.

We start from the level your child is at right now and never compare them with classmates. The goal
is that school lessons already feel clearer within a few weeks.
