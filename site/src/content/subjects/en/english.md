School English in Israel asks for both fluency and precision in written tasks. We combine the two:
your child speaks during the lesson, and grammar appears exactly where it is needed to express an
idea.

For students whose main goal is to start speaking there is the SPEAKABLE track, built around
conversation practice. In middle and high school we add work on the format of exam questions.
