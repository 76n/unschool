## What lessons cover

Mathterful is a track inside maths for students who need more than a quick revision. We work
through curriculum topics in blocks, solve problems at several levels of difficulty, and discuss
why a particular approach was chosen.

In high school we add work on the bagrut format: typical questions, pacing, and a review of
mistakes from earlier papers.
