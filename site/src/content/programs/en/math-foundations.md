## What lessons cover

The lesson is built around practice: your child solves, explains their reasoning and gets a hint
only when one is needed. We work through arithmetic, operations with numbers, fractions and word
problems, in the order that matches their school curriculum.

If gaps are left from earlier years we go back to them separately, without stopping the main line
of lessons.
