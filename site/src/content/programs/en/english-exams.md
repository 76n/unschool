## What lessons cover

Lessons are built around the format: we look at the parts the exam is made of, what each task
checks and how to divide the time between them.

For most of the lesson your child works with real tasks: reading texts, writing answers and
speaking. After every practice paper we go through the mistakes and return to the topics behind
them.
