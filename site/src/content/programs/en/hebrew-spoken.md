## What lessons cover

The lesson runs on speaking: your child talks about school, friends, plans and everyday things. We
correct mistakes in a way that keeps the conversation going.

Step by step we add the vocabulary needed in class and with friends, and practise listening: a
teacher talking at speed, announcements, short videos.
