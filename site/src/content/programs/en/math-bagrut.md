## What lessons cover

Preparation starts with the level: we look at current grades, how much time is left before the
exam and what your child is aiming for, and only then decide between 3, 4 and 5 units.

After that we work through the exam topics: we break down typical questions, solve whole practice
papers and keep an eye on the clock. Mistakes from earlier papers are reviewed separately, so they
do not come back on the day.
