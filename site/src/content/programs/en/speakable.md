## What lessons cover

SPEAKABLE is a live track inside English built around speaking. For most of the lesson the student
talks: discusses a topic, disagrees, retells, asks questions. Mistakes are corrected in a way that
does not cut the conversation short.

Alongside that we use school texts and assignments, so that confidence in speaking carries over
into written work and exams.
