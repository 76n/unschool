## What lessons cover

In high school Hebrew asks for precision: understand the text, identify what matters and phrase an
answer the way the examiner expects. We break down the structure of the tasks and practise writing
in exam format.

Before lessons start we talk about how much time is left before the exam and what workload is
realistic.
