## What lessons cover

We work with the Hebrew that school demands every day: the teacher's instructions, textbook
passages, answering out loud, written assignments. Vocabulary comes from actual school subjects
rather than abstract word lists.

If your child joined an Israeli school recently, we start with listening comprehension and add
reading and writing step by step.
