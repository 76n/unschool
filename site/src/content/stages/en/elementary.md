In primary school the most important thing is that a child does not settle into the feeling of "I
can't do this". Lessons move in short stretches, change activity often, and always end with
something that worked.

We cover three subjects: arithmetic and word problems in maths, first words and phrases in English,
and following the lesson and reading in Hebrew.
