In middle school the curriculum speeds up and earlier gaps become more visible. At this stage we
spend a lot of time making sure a student can work through a task alone and knows what to do when
something is unclear.

In parallel we prepare for the choice of study level in high school: we see which topics are harder
and strengthen them in advance.
