## Delivery of the service

- **Lessons:** the service is given online and nothing is shipped. Within 2 business days of the payment we agree the lesson times with the family by phone, WhatsApp or Telegram. Paid lessons stay on the balance of the student until they take place.
- **Digital materials:** as soon as the payment is confirmed, a download link is sent by email and shown on the order page. The link works for a limited time and can be requested again.

## Moving and cancelling a lesson

- A lesson cancelled at least 6 hours before the start is not charged and can be moved. A later cancellation or a no show is charged.
- Group lessons cancelled in time stay on the balance for the next month.
- If we cancel a lesson, it is moved to another time or not charged.

## Cancelling a purchase and refunds

- You may cancel a purchase of lessons within 14 days of the payment, as the Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981 allows.

- You may cancel a purchase of a digital material within 14 days as long as the file has not been downloaded. A downloaded material cannot be cancelled, as the law allows for digital information. If something is wrong with the file, we fix it or refund the payment.
- Send the cancellation in writing to hello@unschool.cool or by WhatsApp to +972 51-509-7499, with the name of the buyer, the phone and the purchase details.
- The refund goes to the same payment method within 14 days of receiving the cancellation notice.
