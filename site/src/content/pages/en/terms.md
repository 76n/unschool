## School services

A request for an assessment meeting is not a contract. Lesson format, schedule and price are
agreed after the meeting.

## Digital materials

Buying a digital material grants the right to use it within one family. Reselling, publishing or
passing the file to others is not allowed.

Access to a purchased material is given through a link that works for a limited time. The link can
be requested again.

## Payment

Payment is processed by the payment provider. An order counts as paid only after a server-side
confirmation from the provider.

## Refunds

If a material does not suit you or something is wrong with the file, write to hello@unschool.cool.
We review every refund request and reply by email.

## Contact

Questions about orders and access: hello@unschool.cool.
