Unschool is an online school for school students in Israel. We work on three subjects: maths,
English and Hebrew, from grade 1 to grade 12.

## How we work

We start with a free assessment meeting, because without it no honest recommendation is possible.
After the meeting the parent gets a recommendation and decides for themselves whether to continue.

The teaching language is chosen to suit the child: our teachers work in Hebrew, Russian and
English. The language of the website and the language of lessons are not linked.

## What we do not do

We do not promise results in advance, we do not talk about "guaranteed grades", and we do not
publish testimonials without the author's permission. If something does not exist yet — a recorded
course, a confirmed price — we say so plainly.

## Materials

Alongside lessons we prepare digital materials: workbooks, task sets and recorded courses. They
can be bought separately, without taking lessons.
