## Who we are

We work with students from grade 1 to grade 12 and help in three areas: maths, English and Hebrew. Lessons can be in Russian, Hebrew or English. We choose the language so that your child finds it comfortable to learn, while gradually feeling more confident at school.

Our job is not just to "bring a subject up". First we try to understand where exactly the difficulty started: missing basics, a current topic that does not make sense, the language getting in the way, gaps from earlier years, or simply a child who needs a different pace and a different way of explaining.

## Where learning starts

Getting to know the school starts with a free assessment meeting. The teacher meets your child, watches how they reason and solve problems, and identifies their strengths and gaps. After the meeting parents get recommendations and can decide calmly whether lessons suit them and which format to choose.

If your child stays with us, we build the lessons around their specific goal. That might be help with the school curriculum, closing gaps, exam preparation, working more confidently in Hebrew, or the opposite: getting ahead of the curriculum.

## How lessons work

We try not to turn learning into an endless march through topics "in order". If your child can already do something, there is no point spending time on it. If an earlier gap turns up that blocks progress, we deal with it first.

That said, it is not only the lessons themselves that matter to us. In every format the student gets the materials they need, homework that is checked, and feedback. Parents know what their child is working on right now, what is already going well and what to pay attention to next.

## What matters to us

We do not promise a particular grade or exam result in advance. Too many factors affect the outcome, and it feels wrong to us to sell lessons through guarantees that cannot honestly be given. Instead we try to be transparent: we explain the format, the price and the goals of the lessons up front, and we talk about progress regularly.

## Not only lessons

Besides regular lessons, we create our own study materials: workbooks, topic-based tasks and recorded courses. Some of them are used in lessons, and some can be bought separately for independent work.

For us, Unschool is a school where a child does not have to fit into one universal way of learning. We try to find the approach that makes things clearer and calmer for that particular child.
