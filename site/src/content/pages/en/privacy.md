## What we collect

- Assessment request: name, contact details, subject, grade, goal and comment.
- Lesson purchase: first and last name of the payer, phone, country, email, the name of the student and the purchase details.
- Lessons: schedule, attendance, lesson notes and payments.
- Digital material orders: email, name, order contents, amount and payment status.
- Analytics: page views and events, with no name, phone or email.

We do not ask about the health of the child, and the school name is optional.

## Why we use it

We use the data only to contact you, schedule and run the lessons, deliver purchased materials, issue receipts and answer your requests. We do not sell the data or pass it to anyone for advertising.

## Services that process the data for us

Google (hosting and calendar), Zoom, Meta (WhatsApp), Telegram, Morning and Grow (payments and receipts). Each gets only what its job needs.

## Payments

Card details are entered only on the secure page of the payment provider and never reach us. We do not store card numbers.

## How we protect the data

- The data is stored on Google Cloud servers and encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.
- Only school staff signed in with their own account can reach it, and every change is logged.
- Teachers see only the names of their students, never phone numbers.

## How long we keep it

Data is kept while you study with us. Financial records are kept for 7 years as Israeli tax law requires. Everything else is deleted within 12 months after the last lesson or request.

## Your rights

You may ask to see, correct, export or delete your data at any time. Write to hello@unschool.cool.
