> launch.

## What we collect

- Data from the assessment request: name, contact details, subject, grade, goal and comment.
- Order data for digital materials: email address, order contents, amount and payment status.
- Technical analytics data: page views and events, with no personal data attached.

We do not ask for information about a child's health, and we do not require the school name.

## Why we use it

Request data is needed to contact you and prepare the meeting. Order data is needed to deliver the
purchased material, send a receipt and help if delivery goes wrong.

## Payments

Card details are handled by the payment provider. We do not store card numbers on our servers.

## Storage and access

Requests and orders are kept in a protected database that only school staff can access. Analytics
events contain no name, phone number or email address.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Write to hello@unschool.cool.
