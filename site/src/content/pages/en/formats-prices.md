We only show prices the school has confirmed. Where a program price is not fixed yet, the program
page says that the price is confirmed after the assessment — we do not invent approximate numbers.

## What a lesson includes

- A lesson with the teacher at the scheduled time
- The materials needed during the lesson itself
- A short note to the parent about what was covered

## What it does not include

- Digital materials from the catalogue — they are sold separately
- Extra lessons outside the schedule
- Any support the school has not confirmed separately

Payment for lessons is agreed after the assessment. Lessons cannot be paid for on the site yet —
only digital materials can be bought here.
