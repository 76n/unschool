## Step 1. Request

You fill in a short form: subject, grade, goal. If you are not sure about the level or the format,
choose "I don't know" — that is perfectly fine, and exactly what the assessment is for.

## Step 2. Free assessment meeting

The meeting is online and takes about 30 minutes. A teacher talks with your child, sets a few
tasks and sees where the difficulty begins. Parents are welcome to join.

We do not grade your child and do not compare them with anyone. The point of the meeting is to
work out where to start.

## Step 3. Recommendation

After the meeting we send a recommendation: subject, program, format (group or one to one) and an
idea of the schedule. You decide whether to start.

## Step 4. Lessons

Lessons run online on a fixed schedule. The teacher tells you what was covered and what is worth
revisiting at home. If the format is not working, we can discuss it and change it.

## What you need for a lesson

- A computer or tablet with a camera
- Headphones and a quiet place
- A notebook and pen — for maths and Hebrew
