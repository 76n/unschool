## Step 1. Request

Fill in a short form and tell us about your child: which subject interests them, which grade they
are in and what they need help with. It lets the teacher prepare for the assessment.

## Step 2. Free assessment meeting

The meeting is online. The teacher gets to know your child, reviews their knowledge in full and
identifies their strengths and main gaps.

## Step 3. Recommendation

After the meeting we go through the results with you and give our recommendations: where it is
best to start, which topics to focus on, and which format and schedule suit your child.

## Step 4. Lessons

Lessons run online against a personal study plan. The teacher marks homework, tracks progress and
gives parents regular feedback. The plan and the format are adjusted whenever that is needed.

## What you need for lessons

- A computer or tablet with a camera and microphone
- A stable internet connection
- A quiet place to study
- A notebook and pen
