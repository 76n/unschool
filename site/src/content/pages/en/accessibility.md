We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 AA.

## What is already in place

- Semantic headings and page landmarks
- Keyboard operation with a visible focus indicator
- Text alternatives for meaningful images
- Full right-to-left support for Hebrew
- Form error messages that do not rely on colour alone

## How we check the site

Before every significant update we go through the main journeys by hand on mobile devices, with a
keyboard and with screen readers. An automated check in three browsers runs on every change to the
site and makes sure menus and buttons are not cut off on narrow screens or with enlarged text.

## Feedback

If something makes the site hard to use, write to hello@unschool.cool and describe the page and
your device. We will reply, fix the problem, and in the meantime help you get the information
another way.
