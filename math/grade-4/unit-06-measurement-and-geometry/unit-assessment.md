**41:**
Maria's classmate is correct. A "right rhombus" would be a rhombus (4 equal sides) with a right angle. In a rhombus, opposite angles are equal, so a right angle would mean all four angles are 90° (since if one angle is 90°, the opposite angle is also 90°, and adjacent angles are supplementary, so the remaining two angles would also be 90°). A shape with 4 equal sides and 4 right angles is, by definition, a square.
(2 pts: 1 for identifying the classmate as correct; 1 for a valid explanation involving the properties — equal sides + right angles = square)
