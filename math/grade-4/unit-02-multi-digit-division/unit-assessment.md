### Section 4

**17.** 85 ÷ 6 = 14 R1. **14 pieces** can be cut. (Ignore remainder — 1 inch isn't enough for another piece.)

**18.** 215 ÷ 9 = 23 R8. **24 buses** are needed. (Round up — the 8 remaining students need a bus.)

**19.** 135 ÷ 8 = 16 R7. Each child gets **16 stickers**. **7 stickers** are left over.

**20.** 250 ÷ 9 = 27 R7. **7 muffins** will be left unpacked. (Remainder is the answer.)

### Section 5

**21.**
Plan: Total pencils = 6 × 144, then divide by 8.
Step 1: 6 × 144 = 864
Step 2: 864 ÷ 8 = **108 pencils per classroom**

**22.**
Plan: Total money = $245 + $55, then divide by $6.
Step 1: $245 + $55 = $300
Step 2: $300 ÷ $6 = **50 books**, **$0 left over**

**23.**
Plan: Total plants = 5 × 48, subtract 18, then divide by 3.
Step 1: 5 × 48 = 240
Step 2: 240 − 18 = 222
Step 3: 222 ÷ 3 = **74 plants per section**

## Scoring Guide

| Score | Level | Action |
|-------|-------|--------|
| 48-60 | Mastery | Proceed to Unit 3 |
| 36-47 | Approaching | Reteach weak areas, then reassess those sections |
| Below 36 | Below expectations | Reteach Lessons 4-9 with additional concrete modeling |

### Common patterns to watch:

- **Errors in Section 2 (no remainder):** Likely a procedural issue with the algorithm. Reteach D-M-S-B with grid paper.
- **Errors in Section 3 (with remainder):** Check whether remainders ≥ divisor. Review the check formula.
- **Errors in Section 4 (interpreting):** Student may be computing correctly but not reading the context. Practice the sorting strategy from Lesson 9.
- **Errors in Section 5 (multi-step):** Student may struggle with planning. Practice identifying operations before computing.
