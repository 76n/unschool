## Common Error Patterns to Watch For

| Error | Likely Lesson Gap | Suggested Intervention |
|-------|------------------|----------------------|
| Uses l × w instead of l × w × h | Lesson 1–2 concept | Re-build with unit cubes; review layer strategy |
| Writes "square units" instead of "cubic units" | Lesson 1 | Side-by-side area vs. volume comparison |
| Reverses (x, y) in plotted points | Lessons 6–7 | Trace from origin: "right then up" every time |
| Forgets to convert mixed numbers before multiplying | Lesson 4 | Review improper fraction conversion |
| Counts composite shape's shared portion twice | Lesson 5 | Color-code the two prisms; physically separate them |
| Rule in problem 19 — misses the "+6" component | Lesson 8 | Re-examine table: what is y when x = 0? |
