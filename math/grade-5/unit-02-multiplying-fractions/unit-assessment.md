## Answer Key

### Section 1: Fraction x Whole Number

**1.** 3/4 x 6 = 18/4 = 9/2 = **4 1/2**
(3 x 6 = 18, keep denominator 4. 18/4 = 9/2 = 4 1/2)

**2.** 5 x 2/3 = 10/3 = **3 1/3**
(5 x 2 = 10, keep denominator 3. 10/3 = 3 1/3)

**3.** 7 x 3/8 = 21/8 = **2 5/8**
(7 x 3 = 21, keep denominator 8. 21/8 = 2 5/8)

**4.** 5/6 x 9 = 45/6 = 15/2 = **7 1/2**
(5 x 9 = 45, keep denominator 6. 45/6 simplify by 3 = 15/2 = 7 1/2)

**5.** 2/5 x 4 = 8/5 = **1 3/5 cups**
(2 x 4 = 8, keep denominator 5. 8/5 = 1 3/5)

### Section 2: Fraction x Fraction

**6.** 1/3 x 2/5 = **2/15**
(1 x 2 = 2, 3 x 5 = 15)

**7.** 3/4 x 2/7 = 6/28 = **3/14**
(3 x 2 = 6, 4 x 7 = 28. GCF of 6 and 28 is 2. 6/28 = 3/14)

**8.** 5/6 x 3/10 = 15/60 = **1/4**
(5 x 3 = 15, 6 x 10 = 60. GCF of 15 and 60 is 15. 15/60 = 1/4)

**9.** 4/9 x 3/8 = 12/72 = **1/6**
(4 x 3 = 12, 9 x 8 = 72. GCF of 12 and 72 is 12. 12/72 = 1/6)

**10.** 2/3 x 5/6 = 10/18 = **5/9**
(2 x 5 = 10, 3 x 6 = 18. GCF of 10 and 18 is 2. 10/18 = 5/9)

**11.** 3/4 x 2/3 = 6/12 = **1/2**
The rectangle has 4 x 3 = 12 equal parts total. Shading 3 of the 4 rows and 2 of the 3 columns gives an overlap of 3 x 2 = 6 parts. 6 out of 12 = 6/12 = 1/2.

**12.** The rectangle is divided into fifths (5 rows) and thirds (3 columns), giving 5 x 3 = 15 total parts. With 5 parts shaded, the overlap region is 1 full column out of 3 columns and all 5 rows out of 5 rows.
The multiplication problem is: **1/3 x 5/5 = 5/15 = 1/3**.
Equivalently, students may write **5/5 x 1/3 = 5/15 = 1/3**.
Award full credit for any correct equation that uses the grid divisions and produces 5/15 = 1/3.

### Section 3: Simplifying Before Multiplying

**13.** 3/8 x 4/9
Cross-cancel: 3 and 9 share factor 3 (3 -> 1, 9 -> 3). 4 and 8 share factor 4 (4 -> 1, 8 -> 2).
1/2 x 1/3 = **1/6**

**14.** 5/6 x 3/10
Cross-cancel: 5 and 10 share factor 5 (5 -> 1, 10 -> 2). 3 and 6 share factor 3 (3 -> 1, 6 -> 2).
1/2 x 1/2 = **1/4**

**15.** 7/12 x 8/21
Cross-cancel: 7 and 21 share factor 7 (7 -> 1, 21 -> 3). 8 and 12 share factor 4 (8 -> 2, 12 -> 3).
1/3 x 2/3 = **2/9**

**16.**
a) Without cross-canceling: 6/7 x 14/15 = 84/105. GCF of 84 and 105 is 21. 84/105 = **4/5**
b) With cross-canceling: 6 and 15 share factor 3 (6 -> 2, 15 -> 5). 14 and 7 share factor 7 (14 -> 2, 7 -> 1). 2/1 x 2/5 = **4/5**
c) Yes, both methods give 4/5.

### Section 4: Mixed Numbers

**17.** 2 1/3 x 6
Convert: 2 1/3 = 7/3.
7/3 x 6 = 42/3 = **14**

**18.** 1 3/4 x 2/5
Convert: 1 3/4 = 7/4.
7/4 x 2/5 = 14/20 = 7/10 = **7/10**

**19.** 3 1/2 x 2 2/3
Convert: 3 1/2 = 7/2. 2 2/3 = 8/3.
7/2 x 8/3 = 56/6 = 28/3 = **9 1/3**

**20.** 1 4/5 x 2 1/2
Estimate: about 2 x 2 1/2 = 5, or about 2 x 3 = 6. Accept any reasonable estimate in the range of 4-5.
Convert: 1 4/5 = 9/5. 2 1/2 = 5/2.
9/5 x 5/2 = 45/10 = 9/2 = **4 1/2**
Yes, 4 1/2 is close to the estimate of about 5.

### Section 5: Scaling, Reasoning, and Word Problems

**21.** 3/4 x 28 **<** 28
Reasoning: Multiplying by 3/4 (a fraction less than 1) makes the number smaller.

**22.** 7/7 x 15 **=** 15
Reasoning: 7/7 equals 1, and multiplying by 1 does not change the number.

**23.** 5/3 x 12 **>** 12
Reasoning: 5/3 is greater than 1 (it equals 1 2/3), so multiplying by it makes the number larger.

**24.** 3/4 x 2/3 = 6/12 = **1/2 square meter**

**25.** 2/5 x 3 1/2
Convert: 3 1/2 = 7/2.
2/5 x 7/2 = 14/10 = 7/5 = **1 2/5 miles**

**26.**
Step 1: 2/3 x 2 1/4
Convert: 2 1/4 = 9/4.
2/3 x 9/4 = 18/12 = 3/2 = **1 1/2 cups** total.
Step 2: 1 1/2 - 1 = **1/2 cup** more to add.

### Partial credit guidelines

- For computation problems (Sections 1-4): Award 1 point for correct process with an arithmetic error if the method is clearly shown. Deduct 0.5 points for a correct answer that is not simplified to lowest terms.
- For area model problems: Award 1 point for a correct drawing/interpretation even if the written product has an arithmetic error.
- For word problems: Award 1 point for correct setup (equation) even if the final computation contains an error.
- For scaling comparisons: Both the correct symbol and a valid reasoning statement are required for full credit. Correct symbol alone earns 1 of 2 points.

### Common patterns to watch:

- **Errors in Section 1 (fraction x whole number):** Student may be multiplying both numerator and denominator by the whole number. Reteach Lesson 1 — write the whole number as n/1 first, then multiply.
- **Errors in Section 2 (fraction x fraction):** Student may be adding denominators instead of multiplying them, or finding common denominators (confusing with addition). Reteach Lesson 5 with area model support from Lesson 4.
- **Errors in Section 3 (cross-canceling):** Student may be canceling a numerator with its own denominator instead of cross-diagonally, or applying canceling to addition problems. Reteach Lesson 6 with color-coded cross-diagonal pairs.
- **Errors in Section 4 (mixed numbers):** Student may forget to convert to improper fractions, or may multiply whole parts and fraction parts separately and incorrectly combine them. Reteach Lesson 7 — enforce the explicit conversion step.
- **Errors in Section 5 (scaling/word problems):** For scaling, student may compute instead of reasoning about the size of the factor. For word problems, student may confuse multiplication with division or set up the wrong operation. Reteach Lesson 9 (scaling reasoning) and Lesson 10 (identifying key phrases like "of").
- **Not simplifying:** Consistent failure to simplify across all sections indicates the student needs targeted practice on finding GCFs and recognizing common factors. Revisit simplifying from Unit 1 and Lesson 6 of this unit.
