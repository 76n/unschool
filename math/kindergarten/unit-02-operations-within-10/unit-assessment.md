# Unit Assessment — Kindergarten Math, Unit 2: Addition and Subtraction Within 10

**Grade:** Kindergarten
**Unit:** 2 — Addition and Subtraction Within 10
**Assessment Type:** Summative (unit-level)
**Administration:** One-on-one with facilitator (oral and hands-on)
**Estimated Duration:** 20-25 minutes
**Materials:** 10 counting objects (two colors), ten-frame, paper and pencil, sign cards (+, −, =), number cards 0-10

---

## Assessment Overview

| Section | Skill Assessed | Lesson Source | Items |
|---------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| A | Addition with objects | Lessons 1-3 | 3 |
| B | Subtraction with objects | Lessons 5-6 | 3 |
| C | Number sentence reading and writing | Lessons 4, 7 | 4 |
| D | Story problems | Lesson 8 | 3 |
| E | Cumulative review (Unit 1) | Unit 1 | 2 |
| — | **Total** | — | **15** |

**Mastery threshold:** 85% overall (13 of 15 items correct) AND at least 1 correct item in each section.

---

## Administration Instructions

The student may use objects, drawings, or the ten-frame for any problem. Encourage the use of tools — this assessment measures understanding, not memorization. Read story problems aloud. Record responses.

---

## Section A: Addition with Objects

**A1.** "Put together 3 objects and 4 objects. How many altogether?" (Answer: 7)

**A2.** "What is 5 and 5?" (Student uses objects or ten-frame.) (Answer: 10)

**A3.** "Name a way to make 7 using two groups." (Accept any valid pair: 1+6, 2+5, 3+4, 4+3, 5+2, 6+1, 7+0, 0+7)

| Item | Correct Response | Score |
|------|-----------------|-------|
| A1 | 7 | ___ / 1 |
| A2 | 10 | ___ / 1 |
| A3 | Any valid pair | ___ / 1 |

## Section B: Subtraction with Objects

**B1.** "Start with 8 objects. Take away 3. How many are left?" (Answer: 5)

**B2.** "Start with 6 objects. Take away 6. How many are left?" (Answer: 0)

**B3.** "Start with 10 objects. Take away 4. How many are left?" (Answer: 6)

| Item | Correct Response | Score |
|------|-----------------|-------|
| B1 | 5 | ___ / 1 |
| B2 | 0 | ___ / 1 |
| B3 | 6 | ___ / 1 |

## Section C: Number Sentences

**C1.** Show: 4 + 3 = 7. "Read this number sentence aloud."

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Reads correctly ("four plus three equals seven") | 1 point |
| Cannot read or reads incorrectly | 0 points |

**C2.** Show: 9 − 2 = 7. "Read this number sentence aloud."

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Reads correctly ("nine minus two equals seven") | 1 point |
| Cannot read or reads incorrectly | 0 points |

**C3.** "I'm going to show you 5 objects and add 2 more. Write the number sentence." (Answer: 5 + 2 = 7)

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Writes a correct addition sentence | 1 point |
| Incorrect sentence or cannot write | 0 points |

**C4.** "I'm going to start with 7 objects and take away 3. Write the number sentence." (Answer: 7 − 3 = 4)

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Writes a correct subtraction sentence | 1 point |
| Incorrect sentence or cannot write | 0 points |

---

## Section D: Story Problems

Read each problem aloud. The student may use objects or drawings.

**D1.** "There are 6 birds on a branch. 4 more birds land on the branch. How many birds are on the branch now?" (Answer: 10; addition)

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Correctly identifies addition and gets 10 | 1 point |
| Wrong operation or wrong answer | 0 points |

**D2.** "Sam has 9 stickers. He gives 5 to his friend. How many stickers does Sam have left?" (Answer: 4; subtraction)

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Correctly identifies subtraction and gets 4 | 1 point |
| Wrong operation or wrong answer | 0 points |

**D3.** "Make up your own story problem and solve it."

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Creates a valid story problem with correct solution | 1 point |
| Story doesn't make sense or solution is wrong | 0 points |

## Section E: Cumulative Review (Unit 1)

## Rubric and Mastery Determination

| Level | Score Range | Meaning | Next Steps |
|-------|-----------|---------|------------|
| **Mastered** | 13-15 (85-100%) with ≥1 correct per section | Solid addition and subtraction foundations | Advance to Unit 3: Place Value (Numbers 11-19). Continue spaced review of addition/subtraction. |
| **Approaching** | 11-12 (70-84%) | Most skills solid, with specific gaps | Reteach the section(s) where the student scored 0. Extra practice before advancing. |
| **Developing** | Below 11 (<70%) | Significant gaps in operations | Return to the earliest struggling section. Reteach with additional object-based practice. Do not advance until addition and subtraction are reliable within 10. |

### Section-Specific Reteaching Guide

| If struggling in... | Focus on... |
|---|---|
| Section A (Addition) | Return to Lesson 1 concept, then build to objects. Use ten-frame for sums 6-10. Practice "make 10" pairs. |
| Section B (Subtraction) | Return to Lesson 5 (dramatic take-away stories). Use ten-frame for starting numbers 6-10. Keep it physical. |
| Section C (Number Sentences) | This is a notation issue, not a concept issue. Practice building sentences with cards before writing. Focus on + vs. − recognition. |
| Section D (Story Problems) | Re-read and act out stories. Practice identifying clue words. Separate the "what operation?" decision from the computation. |
| Section E (Unit 1 Review) | Return to Unit 1 materials for targeted review before continuing. |

---

## Facilitator Observations

Note during the assessment:

- Does the student use objects, drawings, or mental strategies? (All are valid; the progression matters over time)
- Can the student distinguish + from − quickly, or do they hesitate?
- Does the student seem confident or anxious? (Watch for signs of math anxiety)
- For story problems, does the student identify the operation before computing, or does they guess?
