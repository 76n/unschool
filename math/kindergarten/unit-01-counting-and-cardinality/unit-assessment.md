# Unit Assessment — Kindergarten Math, Unit 1: Counting and Cardinality

**Grade:** Kindergarten
**Unit:** 1 — Counting and Cardinality
**Assessment Type:** Summative (unit-level)
**Administration:** One-on-one with facilitator (this assessment is oral and hands-on, not a written test)
**Estimated Duration:** 15-20 minutes
**Materials:** 15 counting objects, number cards 0-10, paper and pencil, ten-frame

---

## Assessment Overview

This assessment covers all Unit 1 learning objectives:

| Section | Skill Assessed | Lesson Source | Items |
|---------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| A | Oral counting | Lesson 1 | 2 |
| B | One-to-one correspondence | Lesson 2 | 2 |
| C | Cardinality (how many?) | Lesson 3 | 2 |
| D | Numeral recognition and writing | Lessons 4-5 | 4 |
| E | Comparing groups | Lesson 6 | 3 |
| — | **Total** | — | **13** |

**Mastery threshold:** 85% overall (11 of 13 items correct) AND at least 1 correct item in each section.

---

## Administration Instructions

Read each item aloud. Record the student's response. For hands-on items, observe and note what the student does. There are no time limits — let the student work at their pace.

Do NOT provide hints or corrections during the assessment. If the student is stuck for more than 15 seconds, say: "That's okay — let's try the next one."

Create a comfortable atmosphere. Say: "We're going to do some counting activities together. There are no tricks — just show me what you know. It's okay if some are hard."

## Section A: Oral Counting

**A1.** "Count from 1 to 10 for me."

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Counts 1-10 in correct order without skipping | 1 point |
| Skips a number, reverses order, or stops before 10 | 0 points |

**A2.** "What number comes after 7?"

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| "8" | 1 point |
| Any other answer | 0 points |

---

## Section B: One-to-One Correspondence

**B1.** Place 6 objects in a row. "Count these for me."

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Touches or points to each object exactly once while saying the counting sequence; arrives at 6 | 1 point |
| Double-counts, skips objects, or arrives at wrong total | 0 points |

**B2.** Place 9 objects in a scattered (not lined-up) arrangement. "Count these for me."

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Counts each object exactly once (using any tracking strategy) and arrives at 9 | 1 point |
| Miscounts due to skipping or double-counting | 0 points |

---

## Section C: Cardinality

**C1.** After the student counts the 9 objects in B2, ask: "How many are there?"

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Says "9" without recounting | 1 point |
| Recounts and then says "9" | 0.5 points (round up when totaling) |
| Gives wrong answer | 0 points |

**C2.** Place 5 objects in a row. Student counts them. Then spread them apart widely. Ask: "How many are there now?"

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Says "5" without recounting (or says "still 5") | 1 point |
| Recounts and then says "5" | 0.5 points (round up when totaling) |
| Gives wrong answer | 0 points |

---

## Section D: Numeral Recognition and Writing

**D1.** Show the number card for 4. "What number is this?"

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| "Four" or "4" | 1 point |
| Any other answer | 0 points |

**D2.** Show the number card for 8. "What number is this?"

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| "Eight" or "8" | 1 point |
| Any other answer | 0 points |

**D3.** "Write the number 3."

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Writes a recognizable 3 (may be reversed or imperfect) | 1 point |
| Writes an unrecognizable mark or wrong numeral | 0 points |

**D4.** Show 7 objects. "Count these and write the number."

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Counts correctly AND writes a recognizable 7 | 1 point |
| Counts correctly but writes wrong numeral, or miscounts | 0 points |

---

## Section E: Comparing Groups

**E1.** Place 3 objects on one plate and 7 objects on another. "Which plate has more?"

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Points to or says the plate with 7 | 1 point |
| Points to the plate with 3 or says "I don't know" | 0 points |

**E2.** Place 5 objects on one plate and 5 on another. "Does one plate have more, or are they the same?"

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Says "the same" (or equivalent: "they're equal," "both have 5") | 1 point |
| Says one plate has more | 0 points |

**E3.** Place 8 objects on one plate and 4 on another. "Which plate has fewer?"

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Points to or says the plate with 4 | 1 point |
| Points to the plate with 8 | 0 points |

## Rubric and Mastery Determination

| Level | Score Range | Meaning | Next Steps |
|-------|-----------|---------|------------|
| **Mastered** | 11-13 (85-100%) with at least 1 correct in each section | Student has solid counting and cardinality foundations | Advance to Unit 2: Addition and Subtraction Within 10. Continue spaced review of counting in warm-ups. |
| **Approaching** | 9-10 (70-84%) | Partial understanding — some skills are solid, others need reinforcement | Review the sections where the student scored 0. Reteach those specific lessons (1-2 sessions each) before advancing. |
| **Developing** | Below 9 (<70%) | Significant gaps in foundational counting skills | Return to the earliest section where the student struggled. Reteach from that point forward. Do not advance to Unit 2 yet — these skills must be solid first. |

### Section-Specific Reteaching Guide

| If the student struggles in... | Reteach with... |
|-------------------------------|----------------|
| Section A (Oral Counting) | Daily oral counting practice — counting songs, counting steps, counting objects throughout the day. Repeat Lesson 1. |
| Section B (One-to-One) | Hands-on counting with the "move aside" strategy. Repeat Lesson 2 with sets of 3-5 first, then build up. |
| Section C (Cardinality) | After every counting activity, ask "how many?" and redirect if the student recounts. Repeat Lesson 3. |
| Section D (Numerals) | Daily flash-card practice with number cards. Tracing and writing practice (tactile methods). Repeat Lessons 4-5. |
| Section E (Comparing) | Use the line-up matching strategy with small groups (2 vs. 4). Practice "more/fewer/same" language daily. Repeat Lesson 6. |

## Cumulative Review Items

These items review skills from earlier in the unit and are included to reinforce long-term retention. They are NOT scored as part of the unit assessment but provide useful diagnostic information.

**R1.** "Count backward from 5 to 1." (Can the student reverse the sequence?)

**R2.** Show a ten-frame with 6 filled. "How many?" (Can the student use the ten-frame without counting each dot?)

**R3.** "Show me zero objects." (Does the student understand zero?)

Note the student's responses to these items in your observations — they inform readiness for Unit 2.

---

## Facilitator Observations (Record During Assessment)

Use this space to note qualitative observations:

- Does the student count confidently or hesitantly?
- Which tracking strategy does the student use (touching, moving, pointing)?
- Does the student self-correct errors?
- How does the student hold the pencil? (Fine motor observation)
- Does the student show frustration or anxiety? (Emotional observation)

These notes help inform differentiation decisions in Unit 2.
