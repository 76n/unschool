# Math -- Grade 7 -- Unit 3, Lesson 10

## Topic
Review and Problem Solving

## Learning Objectives
- Simplify expressions using the distributive property and combining like terms.
- Solve two-step equations and equations with variables on both sides.
- Solve inequalities and graph solution sets on a number line.
- Apply all unit skills to mixed and multi-step problems.

## Prerequisites
- All content from Lessons 1-9 of this unit.

## Materials Needed
- Whiteboard and markers (or projector).
- Number lines (for inequality graphing).
- Student worksheet (Guided Practice and Independent Practice problems).
- Index cards or half-sheets for Exit Ticket.
- Calculator (optional).

**Standards:** 7.EE.1, 7.EE.2, 7.EE.3, 7.EE.4

## Warm-Up (5 minutes)

**Purpose:** Quick skill check across all major topics.

Complete each task:
1. Combine like terms: 4x - 3 + 2x + 7 (Answer: 6x + 4)
2. Expand: -2(3y - 5) (Answer: -6y + 10)
3. Solve: 5x + 3 = 28 (Answer: x = 5)
4. Solve: -3x > 9 (Answer: x < -3)

Say: "Today we bring everything together. This is your chance to review all the skills from this unit before the assessment."

## Direct Instruction (10-15 minutes)

### Key Vocabulary
- Review all vocabulary from Lessons 1-9 as needed.

### Instruction Notes

**Step 1 -- Review the skill categories.**

Say: "Let me organize what we have learned in this unit into four categories."

Write on the board:

| Category | Key Skills | Lessons |
|---|---|---|
| Simplifying Expressions | Combine like terms, distributive property, multi-step simplification | 1-4 |
| Solving Equations | Two-step equations, variables on both sides | 5-6 |
| Word Problems with Equations | Define variable, translate, solve, interpret | 7 |
| Inequalities | Solve, sign-flip rule, graph, word problems | 8-9 |

**Step 2 -- Work through a multi-step problem from each category.**

Say: "Let me demonstrate one problem from each category, showing all the steps."

**Expression Problem:** Simplify 3(2x - 4) - 2(x + 5)
- Distribute: 6x - 12 - 2x - 10
- Combine: (6x - 2x) + (-12 - 10) = 4x - 22
- Answer: **4x - 22**

**Equation Problem:** Solve 4(x + 3) = 2x + 20
- Distribute: 4x + 12 = 2x + 20
- Subtract 2x: 2x + 12 = 20
- Subtract 12: 2x = 8
- Divide by 2: x = 4
- Check: 4(4 + 3) = 4(7) = 28. And 2(4) + 20 = 8 + 20 = 28. Correct.
- Answer: **x = 4**

**Word Problem:** A tutoring center charges $20 per session plus a $50 registration fee. If a student's family has spent $210, how many sessions has the student attended?
- Let s = sessions. 20s + 50 = 210. 20s = 160. s = 8.
- Answer: **8 sessions**. Check: 20(8) + 50 = 210.

**Inequality Problem:** Solve and graph -3x + 7 ≤ 22
- -3x ≤ 15
- x ≥ -5 (flip the sign)
- Graph: Closed circle at -5, shade right.
- Answer: **x ≥ -5**

**Step 3 -- Common errors to watch for.**

Say: "Let me highlight the most common mistakes I see across all these skills."

| Error | Correction |
|---|---|
| Combining unlike terms (3x + 5y = 8xy) | Like terms must have the same variable and exponent. |
| Distributing to only one term: 4(x + 3) = 4x + 3 | Distribute to EVERY term inside parentheses. |
| Wrong order in equation solving: dividing before subtracting | Undo addition/subtraction FIRST, then multiplication/division. |
| Forgetting to flip inequality sign when dividing by negative | Always flip when multiplying or dividing by a negative number. |
| Not checking solutions | Always substitute back into the ORIGINAL equation or inequality. |

### Worked Examples

**Example A: Mixed simplification and equation**
Solve: 2(x + 3) + x = 21

Step 1 -- Simplify the left side: 2x + 6 + x = 3x + 6
Step 2 -- Solve: 3x + 6 = 21. 3x = 15. x = 5.
Check: 2(5 + 3) + 5 = 2(8) + 5 = 16 + 5 = 21. Correct.
Answer: **x = 5**

**Example B: Inequality with distribution**
Solve and graph: 2(x - 3) < 10

Step 1 -- Distribute: 2x - 6 < 10
Step 2 -- Add 6: 2x < 16
Step 3 -- Divide by 2: x < 8
Graph: Open circle at 8, shade left.
Answer: **x < 8**

**Example C: Word problem requiring inequality**
A parking lot charges $5 per hour. You arrive with $22. For how many complete hours can you park?

Let h = hours. 5h ≤ 22. h ≤ 4.4. Since you need complete hours: h ≤ 4.
Answer: **At most 4 hours**. Check: 5(4) = 20 ≤ 22. And 5(5) = 25 > 22.

## Guided Practice (10 minutes)

1. Simplify: 5(x - 2) + 3(2x + 4)
   - 5x - 10 + 6x + 12 = **11x + 2**
   - **Facilitator tip:** "Distribute both, then combine."

2. Solve: 7x - 4 = 3x + 16
   - 4x = 20, x = **5**. Check: 7(5) - 4 = 31, 3(5) + 16 = 31.
   - **Facilitator tip:** "Move the 3x to the left side by subtracting."

3. Solve: 3(2x + 1) = 27
   - 6x + 3 = 27. 6x = 24. x = **4**. Check: 3(2(4) + 1) = 3(9) = 27.
   - **Facilitator tip:** "You can distribute first, or divide both sides by 3 first."

4. Solve and graph: -5x + 2 > -13
   - -5x > -15. x < 3 (flip). Open circle at 3, shade left.
   - **Facilitator tip:** "Subtract 2 from both sides, then divide by -5 and flip."

5. A theater has 300 seats. Tickets cost $12 each. The theater has already sold 180 tickets. How many more tickets must be sold to earn at least $3,000 in total?
   - Total revenue = 12(180 + t) where t = additional tickets. 12(180 + t) ≥ 3000. 2160 + 12t ≥ 3000. 12t ≥ 840. t ≥ 70. **At least 70 more tickets**.
   - **Facilitator tip:** "The total tickets sold is 180 + t."

## Independent Practice (10-15 minutes)

### Part A: Simplifying Expressions

1. Combine like terms: -3a + 7b + 5a - 2b
2. Expand and simplify: 4(2x - 3) + 5x + 1
3. Expand and simplify: -2(3y + 1) + 5(y - 4)

### Part B: Solving Equations

4. Solve: 6x + 7 = -11
5. Solve: -8x - 3 = 21
6. Solve: 9x - 5 = 4x + 20
7. Solve: 2(x + 6) = 3x + 8

### Part C: Solving Inequalities

8. Solve and graph: 4x + 9 < 25
9. Solve and graph: -6x - 2 ≥ 16
10. Solve and graph: 3(x - 2) > 12

### Part D: Word Problems

11. A bike rental shop charges $7 per hour plus a $10 helmet fee. You paid $38. How many hours did you rent the bike?
12. You are saving for a $150 video game console. You already have $45 and save $15 per week. How many weeks until you have enough?
13. A bakery needs to bake at least 200 cupcakes for an order. They can bake 24 cupcakes per batch. They have already baked 56 cupcakes. How many more full batches do they need?

### Answer Key

**Part A**
1. (-3a + 5a) + (7b - 2b) = **2a + 5b**
2. 8x - 12 + 5x + 1 = **13x - 11**
3. -6y - 2 + 5y - 20 = **-y - 22** (or equivalently -1y - 22)

**Part B**
4. 6x + 7 = -11. 6x = -18. x = **-3**. Check: 6(-3) + 7 = -18 + 7 = -11.
5. -8x - 3 = 21. -8x = 24. x = **-3**. Check: -8(-3) - 3 = 24 - 3 = 21.
6. 9x - 5 = 4x + 20. 5x - 5 = 20. 5x = 25. x = **5**. Check: 9(5) - 5 = 40, 4(5) + 20 = 40.
7. 2(x + 6) = 3x + 8. 2x + 12 = 3x + 8. 12 = x + 8. x = **4**. Check: 2(4 + 6) = 2(10) = 20, 3(4) + 8 = 20.

**Part C**
8. 4x + 9 < 25. 4x < 16. x < **4**. Open circle at 4, shade left.
9. -6x - 2 ≥ 16. -6x ≥ 18. x ≤ **-3** (flip). Closed circle at -3, shade left.
10. 3(x - 2) > 12. 3x - 6 > 12. 3x > 18. x > **6**. Open circle at 6, shade right.

**Part D**
11. Let h = hours. 7h + 10 = 38. 7h = 28. h = **4 hours**. Check: 7(4) + 10 = 38.
12. Let w = weeks. 15w + 45 ≥ 150. 15w ≥ 105. w ≥ 7. **At least 7 weeks**. Check: 15(7) + 45 = 150 ≥ 150.
13. Let b = additional batches. 24b + 56 ≥ 200. 24b ≥ 144. b ≥ 6. **At least 6 more batches**. Check: 24(6) + 56 = 144 + 56 = 200 ≥ 200.

## Assessment Check

### Exit Ticket
**Problem 1:** Simplify: 3(x + 5) - 2(x - 1)

**Problem 2:** Solve: -2x + 9 = 4x - 3

**Target response:**
1. 3x + 15 - 2x + 2 = **x + 17**
2. 9 = 6x - 3. 12 = 6x. x = **2**. Check: -2(2) + 9 = 5, 4(2) - 3 = 5.

**Scoring:**
- 2 out of 2 correct = Ready for the unit assessment.
- 1 out of 2 correct = Review the missed skill category before the assessment.
- 0 out of 2 correct = Extended review needed; identify specific gaps using guided practice results.

## Extension
- **Challenge Equation:** Solve 5(2x - 3) - 3(x + 4) = 2x + 5. (10x - 15 - 3x - 12 = 2x + 5. 7x - 27 = 2x + 5. 5x = 32. x = 32/5 = 6.4.) Check: 5(2(6.4) - 3) - 3(6.4 + 4) = 5(9.8) - 3(10.4) = 49 - 31.2 = 17.8. And 2(6.4) + 5 = 12.8 + 5 = 17.8.
- **Error Analysis:** A student solved -4x + 8 > 20 and got x > -3. Find and correct the error. (The student forgot to flip the inequality sign when dividing by -4. Correct: -4x > 12, so x < -3.)
