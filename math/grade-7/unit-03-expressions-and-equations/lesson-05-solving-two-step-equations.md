# Math -- Grade 7 -- Unit 3, Lesson 5

## Topic
Solving Two-Step Equations

## Learning Objectives
- Solve equations of the form ax + b = c using inverse operations in the correct order.
- Explain the "undo" strategy: undo addition/subtraction first, then undo multiplication/division.
- Verify solutions by substituting back into the original equation.
- Solve two-step equations involving negative numbers and rational number solutions.

## Prerequisites
- Ability to solve one-step equations (Grade 6): x + a = b and ax = b.
- Fluency with all four operations on integers, including negative numbers (Unit 2).
- Understanding of inverse operations (addition/subtraction are inverses; multiplication/division are inverses).

## Materials Needed
- Whiteboard and markers (or projector).
- Balance scale diagram (drawn or physical model; for conceptual introduction).
- Student worksheet (Guided Practice and Independent Practice problems).
- Index cards or half-sheets for Exit Ticket.

**Standards:** 7.EE.3 -- Solve multi-step real-life and mathematical problems posed with positive and negative rational numbers in any form. 7.EE.4a -- Solve word problems leading to equations of the form px + q = r and p(x + q) = r, where p, q, and r are specific rational numbers.

## Warm-Up (5 minutes)

**Purpose:** Review one-step equation solving and activate the concept of inverse operations.

1. Solve: x + 8 = 15. (Subtract 8 from both sides: x = 7)
2. Solve: 3x = 24. (Divide both sides by 3: x = 8)
3. Ask: "What if both operations are in the same equation? For example, 3x + 8 = 23. That is a two-step equation, and that is what we learn to solve today."

## Direct Instruction (10-15 minutes)

### Key Vocabulary
- **Two-step equation** -- an equation that requires two operations to isolate the variable. Example: 2x + 5 = 13.
- **Inverse operations** -- operations that undo each other. Addition and subtraction are inverses. Multiplication and division are inverses.
- **Isolate the variable** -- perform operations on both sides of the equation until the variable is alone on one side.

### Instruction Notes

**Step 1 -- Introduce the "undo" strategy with a conceptual model.**

Say: "Think of building the expression 2x + 5. You start with x, multiply by 2, then add 5. To solve 2x + 5 = 13, we undo those operations in REVERSE order."

Write on the board:
```
Building the expression:   x → multiply by 2 → add 5 → result is 13
Solving (undo in reverse):  x ← divide by 2 ← subtract 5 ← start with 13
```

Say: "Undo the LAST operation first. The last operation was 'add 5,' so we subtract 5 from both sides. Then undo the multiplication by dividing both sides by 2."

**Step 2 -- Solve a two-step equation step by step.**

Write: 2x + 5 = 13

Say: "Step 1 -- Undo the addition. Subtract 5 from both sides."
- 2x + 5 - 5 = 13 - 5
- 2x = 8

Say: "Step 2 -- Undo the multiplication. Divide both sides by 2."
- 2x / 2 = 8 / 2
- x = 4

Say: "Step 3 -- Check. Substitute x = 4 into the original equation."
- 2(4) + 5 = 8 + 5 = 13. Correct.

**Step 3 -- Solve an equation with subtraction.**

Write: 4x - 7 = 17

Say: "The operations applied to x are: multiply by 4, then subtract 7. To solve, we undo in reverse: add 7, then divide by 4."

- Step 1: 4x - 7 + 7 = 17 + 7 → 4x = 24
- Step 2: 4x / 4 = 24 / 4 → x = 6
- Check: 4(6) - 7 = 24 - 7 = 17. Correct.

**Step 4 -- Solve an equation with a negative result.**

Write: 3x + 10 = 1

Say: "Follow the same process. Subtract 10 from both sides."
- 3x + 10 - 10 = 1 - 10
- 3x = -9

Say: "Divide both sides by 3."
- x = -9 / 3
- x = -3

Say: "Check: 3(-3) + 10 = -9 + 10 = 1. Correct. The solution is a negative number, and that is perfectly fine."

**Step 5 -- Solve an equation with a negative coefficient.**

Write: -5x + 2 = 17

Say: "Subtract 2 from both sides."
- -5x = 15

Say: "Divide both sides by -5."
- x = 15 / (-5)
- x = -3

Say: "Check: -5(-3) + 2 = 15 + 2 = 17. Correct."

### Worked Examples

**Example A: Standard two-step equation**
Solve: 6x + 3 = 27

Step 1: 6x + 3 - 3 = 27 - 3 → 6x = 24
Step 2: x = 24 / 6 → x = **4**
Check: 6(4) + 3 = 24 + 3 = 27.

**Example B: Equation with subtraction and a non-integer solution**
Solve: 5x - 8 = 12

Step 1: 5x - 8 + 8 = 12 + 8 → 5x = 20
Step 2: x = 20 / 5 → x = **4**
Check: 5(4) - 8 = 20 - 8 = 12.

**Example C: Equation with negative coefficient**
Solve: -4x + 9 = -3

Step 1: -4x + 9 - 9 = -3 - 9 → -4x = -12
Step 2: x = -12 / (-4) → x = **3**
Check: -4(3) + 9 = -12 + 9 = -3.

## Guided Practice (10 minutes)

1. Solve: 3x + 7 = 22
   - 3x = 15, x = **5**. Check: 3(5) + 7 = 22.
   - **Facilitator tip:** "What do you undo first -- the addition or the multiplication?"

2. Solve: 2x - 9 = 5
   - 2x = 14, x = **7**. Check: 2(7) - 9 = 14 - 9 = 5.
   - **Facilitator tip:** "Add 9 to both sides first."

3. Solve: 7x + 4 = -10
   - 7x = -14, x = **-2**. Check: 7(-2) + 4 = -14 + 4 = -10.
   - **Facilitator tip:** "Do not be alarmed by the negative answer. Check it."

4. Solve: -3x + 1 = 16
   - -3x = 15, x = **-5**. Check: -3(-5) + 1 = 15 + 1 = 16.
   - **Facilitator tip:** "Divide by -3. Negative divided by negative is positive -- wait, 15 / (-3) = -5."

5. Solve: 8x - 5 = -5
   - 8x = 0, x = **0**. Check: 8(0) - 5 = 0 - 5 = -5.
   - **Facilitator tip:** "Zero is a valid solution."

6. Solve: 5x + 12 = 2
   - 5x = -10, x = **-2**. Check: 5(-2) + 12 = -10 + 12 = 2.
   - **Facilitator tip:** "12 - 12 = 0 on the left. 2 - 12 = -10 on the right."

## Independent Practice (10-15 minutes)

### Part A: Standard Two-Step Equations

1. 4x + 3 = 19
2. 7x - 2 = 26
3. 9x + 5 = 5
4. 6x - 11 = 1
5. 2x + 15 = 9
6. 10x - 4 = -34

### Part B: Negative Coefficients

7. -2x + 8 = 14
8. -6x - 3 = 21
9. -x + 5 = 12

### Part C: Word Problems

10. A gym membership costs $25 per month plus a one-time registration fee of $40. If you have spent $215 total, how many months have you been a member? Write and solve an equation.
11. Maya is saving money. She already has $18 in her account and saves $7 per week. How many weeks until she has $67? Write and solve an equation.
12. The temperature is dropping at a rate of 3 degrees per hour. The current temperature is 2 degrees. How many hours ago was the temperature 20 degrees? (Hint: 2 - 3h represents the temperature h hours from now, but think about going backward.)

### Answer Key

**Part A**
1. 4x + 3 = 19 → 4x = 16 → x = **4**. Check: 4(4) + 3 = 19.
2. 7x - 2 = 26 → 7x = 28 → x = **4**. Check: 7(4) - 2 = 26.
3. 9x + 5 = 5 → 9x = 0 → x = **0**. Check: 9(0) + 5 = 5.
4. 6x - 11 = 1 → 6x = 12 → x = **2**. Check: 6(2) - 11 = 1.
5. 2x + 15 = 9 → 2x = -6 → x = **-3**. Check: 2(-3) + 15 = -6 + 15 = 9.
6. 10x - 4 = -34 → 10x = -30 → x = **-3**. Check: 10(-3) - 4 = -30 - 4 = -34.

**Part B**
7. -2x + 8 = 14 → -2x = 6 → x = **-3**. Check: -2(-3) + 8 = 6 + 8 = 14.
8. -6x - 3 = 21 → -6x = 24 → x = **-4**. Check: -6(-4) - 3 = 24 - 3 = 21.
9. -x + 5 = 12 → -x = 7 → x = **-7**. Check: -(-7) + 5 = 7 + 5 = 12.

**Part C**
10. Equation: 25m + 40 = 215. Solve: 25m = 175, m = **7 months**. Check: 25(7) + 40 = 175 + 40 = 215.
11. Equation: 7w + 18 = 67. Solve: 7w = 49, w = **7 weeks**. Check: 7(7) + 18 = 49 + 18 = 67.
12. Equation: -3h + 2 = 20 (where h is hours ago, but we model going backward as subtracting). Alternatively, think: 2 + 3h = 20 (going backward h hours, temperature was higher). Solve: 3h = 18, h = **6 hours ago**. Check: The temperature 6 hours ago was 2 + 3(6) = 2 + 18 = 20.

## Assessment Check

### Exit Ticket
**Problem 1:** Solve: 5x + 3 = -12

**Problem 2:** Solve: -4x - 7 = 9

**Target response:**
1. 5x = -15, x = **-3**. Check: 5(-3) + 3 = -15 + 3 = -12.
2. -4x = 16, x = **-4**. Check: -4(-4) - 7 = 16 - 7 = 9.

**Scoring:**
- 2 out of 2 correct (with checks) = Mastery demonstrated.
- 1 out of 2 correct = Partial understanding; review the missed equation type.
- 0 out of 2 correct = Reteach needed; return to the "undo" strategy with one-step equations first.

## Extension
- **Fractional Solutions:** Solve 3x + 5 = 14. (Answer: 3x = 9, x = 3. Now try: 4x + 5 = 14. Answer: 4x = 9, x = 9/4 = 2.25.)
- **Design a Problem:** Write a two-step equation whose solution is x = -6. Trade with a partner and solve each other's equation.
