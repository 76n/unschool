# Math -- Grade 7 -- Unit 3, Lesson 4

## Topic
Simplifying Complex Expressions

## Learning Objectives
- Simplify expressions that require both the distributive property and combining like terms.
- Apply correct order of simplification: distribute first, then combine like terms.
- Simplify expressions with multiple sets of parentheses.
- Identify and correct common errors in multi-step simplification.

## Prerequisites
- Ability to combine like terms (Lesson 2).
- Ability to apply the distributive property with variables (Lesson 3).
- Fluency with integer operations, especially multiplication and addition with negative numbers (Unit 2).

## Materials Needed
- Whiteboard and markers (or projector).
- Student worksheet (Guided Practice and Independent Practice problems).
- Index cards or half-sheets for Exit Ticket.
- Colored pencils (optional; for underlining distributed terms in one step and like terms in another).

**Standards:** 7.EE.1 -- Apply properties of operations as strategies to add, subtract, factor, and expand linear expressions with rational coefficients. 7.EE.2 -- Understand that rewriting an expression in different forms in a problem context can shed light on the problem and how the quantities in it are related.

## Warm-Up (5 minutes)

**Purpose:** Review distributing and combining separately before requiring both in one problem.

1. Expand: 3(2x + 4). (Answer: 6x + 12)
2. Combine like terms: 6x + 12 + x - 5. (Answer: 7x + 7)
3. Ask: "What if a single expression requires BOTH steps? That is what we learn today."

## Direct Instruction (10-15 minutes)

### Key Vocabulary
- **Simplify** -- rewrite an expression in its most compact form by distributing and combining all like terms.
- **Multi-step simplification** -- a simplification process that requires more than one type of operation (e.g., distributing AND combining like terms).

### Instruction Notes

**Step 1 -- The two-step process.**

Say: "When you see an expression with both parentheses and additional terms, follow these two steps in order: Step 1 -- Distribute to remove all parentheses. Step 2 -- Combine like terms."

Write: 3(x + 4) + 2x

Say: "Step 1 -- Distribute: 3(x + 4) = 3x + 12. The expression becomes 3x + 12 + 2x."

Say: "Step 2 -- Combine like terms: 3x + 2x = 5x. Constants: 12."

Result: **5x + 12**

Check with x = 3: Original: 3(3 + 4) + 2(3) = 3(7) + 6 = 21 + 6 = 27. Simplified: 5(3) + 12 = 15 + 12 = 27.

**Step 2 -- Expressions with a negative factor and additional terms.**

Write: -2(x + 5) + 8x - 3

Say: "Step 1 -- Distribute: -2(x + 5) = -2x - 10. The expression becomes -2x - 10 + 8x - 3."

Say: "Step 2 -- Combine like terms: x-terms: -2x + 8x = 6x. Constants: -10 - 3 = -13."

Result: **6x - 13**

Check with x = 2: Original: -2(2 + 5) + 8(2) - 3 = -2(7) + 16 - 3 = -14 + 16 - 3 = -1. Simplified: 6(2) - 13 = 12 - 13 = -1.

**Step 3 -- Expressions with two sets of parentheses.**

Write: 4(x + 2) + 3(x - 5)

Say: "Step 1 -- Distribute BOTH sets of parentheses."
- 4(x + 2) = 4x + 8
- 3(x - 5) = 3x - 15
- Full expression: 4x + 8 + 3x - 15

Say: "Step 2 -- Combine like terms."
- x-terms: 4x + 3x = 7x
- Constants: 8 - 15 = -7

Result: **7x - 7**

Check with x = 3: Original: 4(3 + 2) + 3(3 - 5) = 4(5) + 3(-2) = 20 + (-6) = 14. Simplified: 7(3) - 7 = 21 - 7 = 14.

**Step 4 -- Expressions with subtraction before parentheses.**

Write: 5x - 2(x + 4)

Say: "Be careful here. The -2 multiplies everything inside the parentheses."

- Step 1: 5x + (-2)(x) + (-2)(4) = 5x - 2x - 8
- Step 2: (5x - 2x) - 8 = 3x - 8

Result: **3x - 8**

Say: "A common error is to write 5x - 2x + 4, forgetting to distribute the -2 to the 4. Always distribute to EVERY term."

### Worked Examples

**Example A: Single distribution plus extra terms**
Simplify: 2(3y - 1) + 4y + 7

Step 1 -- Distribute: 6y - 2 + 4y + 7
Step 2 -- Combine: (6y + 4y) + (-2 + 7) = 10y + 5
Answer: **10y + 5**

Check with y = 1: 2(3(1) - 1) + 4(1) + 7 = 2(2) + 4 + 7 = 4 + 4 + 7 = 15. And 10(1) + 5 = 15.

**Example B: Two distributions with opposite signs**
Simplify: 5(x + 3) - 2(x - 4)

Step 1 -- Distribute both:
- 5(x + 3) = 5x + 15
- -2(x - 4) = -2x + 8
- Full: 5x + 15 - 2x + 8

Step 2 -- Combine: (5x - 2x) + (15 + 8) = 3x + 23
Answer: **3x + 23**

Check with x = 1: 5(1 + 3) - 2(1 - 4) = 5(4) - 2(-3) = 20 + 6 = 26. And 3(1) + 23 = 26.

**Example C: More complex expression**
Simplify: -3(2a + 1) + 4(a - 3) + 7

Step 1 -- Distribute:
- -3(2a + 1) = -6a - 3
- 4(a - 3) = 4a - 12
- Full: -6a - 3 + 4a - 12 + 7

Step 2 -- Combine: (-6a + 4a) + (-3 - 12 + 7) = -2a + (-8) = -2a - 8
Answer: **-2a - 8**

Check with a = 5: -3(2(5) + 1) + 4(5 - 3) + 7 = -3(11) + 4(2) + 7 = -33 + 8 + 7 = -18. And -2(5) - 8 = -10 - 8 = -18.

## Guided Practice (10 minutes)

1. Simplify: 4(x + 1) + 3x
   - 4x + 4 + 3x = **7x + 4**
   - **Facilitator tip:** "Distribute first, then combine the x-terms."

2. Simplify: -3(y - 2) + y
   - -3y + 6 + y = **-2y + 6**
   - **Facilitator tip:** "What is -3 times -2? That gives you +6."

3. Simplify: 2(x + 5) + 3(x + 1)
   - 2x + 10 + 3x + 3 = **5x + 13**
   - **Facilitator tip:** "Distribute both sets of parentheses before combining."

4. Simplify: 6(a - 3) - 4(a + 2)
   - 6a - 18 - 4a - 8 = **2a - 26**
   - **Facilitator tip:** "Be careful: -4 times +2 is -8, not +8."

5. Simplify: 10 - 3(2x - 1)
   - 10 - 6x + 3 = -6x + 13
   - Answer: **-6x + 13** (or equivalently 13 - 6x)
   - **Facilitator tip:** "The 10 is not being multiplied. Only the terms inside the parentheses get multiplied by -3."

## Independent Practice (10-15 minutes)

### Part A: Single Distribution Plus Terms

1. 5(x + 2) + 3x
2. -2(a + 4) + 7a
3. 3(2y - 3) + y + 8
4. 8 + 4(x - 1)

### Part B: Double Distribution

5. 3(x + 4) + 2(x + 1)
6. 5(y - 2) + 4(y + 3)
7. 6(a + 1) - 3(a - 5)
8. -2(x + 7) - 3(x - 2)
9. 4(2m + 3) - 5(m - 1)

### Part C: Word Problems

10. A phone plan charges a $20 monthly fee plus $0.05 per text message. You also subscribe to a streaming service that costs $10 per month with a $3 discount this month. Write and simplify an expression for your total monthly cost if you send t text messages.
11. Two students collect donations for a fundraiser. Student A collects (2x + 5) dollars from each of 3 donors. Student B collects (x + 8) dollars from each of 4 donors. Write and simplify an expression for the total donations collected.

### Answer Key

**Part A**
1. 5x + 10 + 3x = **8x + 10**
2. -2a - 8 + 7a = **5a - 8**
3. 6y - 9 + y + 8 = **7y - 1**
4. 8 + 4x - 4 = **4x + 4**

**Part B**
5. 3x + 12 + 2x + 2 = **5x + 14**
6. 5y - 10 + 4y + 12 = **9y + 2**
7. 6a + 6 - 3a + 15 = **3a + 21**
8. -2x - 14 - 3x + 6 = **-5x - 8**
9. 8m + 12 - 5m + 5 = **3m + 17**

**Part C**
10. Cost = 20 + 0.05t + (10 - 3) = 20 + 0.05t + 7 = **0.05t + 27** dollars
11. Total = 3(2x + 5) + 4(x + 8) = 6x + 15 + 4x + 32 = **10x + 47** dollars

## Assessment Check

### Exit Ticket
**Problem:** Simplify: 3(2x + 4) - 5(x - 1)

**Target response:**
- 6x + 12 - 5x + 5
- x + 17

Answer: **x + 17**

**Scoring:**
- Correct final answer with distribution shown = Mastery demonstrated.
- Correct distribution but error combining like terms = Partial; review Lesson 2.
- Incorrect distribution (e.g., -5(x - 1) = -5x - 5) = Reteach distributing with negative factors (Lesson 3).

## Extension
- **Triple Distribution:** Simplify 2(x + 3) + 3(2x - 1) - 4(x + 2). (Answer: 2x + 6 + 6x - 3 - 4x - 8 = 4x - 5)
- **Create Your Own:** Write an expression with two sets of parentheses that simplifies to 5x + 10. Share with a partner and verify each other's work.
