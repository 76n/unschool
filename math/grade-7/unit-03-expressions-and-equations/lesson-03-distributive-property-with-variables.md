# Math -- Grade 7 -- Unit 3, Lesson 3

## Topic
Distributive Property with Variables

## Learning Objectives
- Apply the distributive property to expand expressions of the form a(b + c) where a, b, and/or c contain variables.
- Expand expressions with negative factors outside the parentheses.
- Factor simple expressions by identifying a greatest common factor (GCF).
- Verify expansions and factorizations by substituting values for the variable.

## Prerequisites
- Understanding of the distributive property with numbers (Lesson 1).
- Ability to multiply integers, including negative numbers (Unit 2, Lessons 4-5).
- Familiarity with the concept of terms and coefficients (Lesson 1).

## Materials Needed
- Whiteboard and markers (or projector).
- Algebra tiles (optional but recommended for area model representation).
- Student worksheet (Guided Practice and Independent Practice problems).
- Index cards or half-sheets for Exit Ticket.

**Standards:** 7.EE.1 -- Apply properties of operations as strategies to add, subtract, factor, and expand linear expressions with rational coefficients.

## Warm-Up (5 minutes)

**Purpose:** Review the distributive property with numbers and transition to variables.

1. Expand: 3(7 + 2). Compute both ways: 3(9) = 27; and 3(7) + 3(2) = 21 + 6 = 27.
2. Expand: 5(10 - 4). Both ways: 5(6) = 30; and 5(10) - 5(4) = 50 - 20 = 30.
3. Now try: 4(x + 3). "You cannot add x + 3 first because you do not know what x is. But you CAN distribute the 4."

## Direct Instruction (10-15 minutes)

### Key Vocabulary
- **Expand** -- use the distributive property to remove parentheses and write an equivalent expression. Example: 3(x + 5) expands to 3x + 15.
- **Factor** -- write an expression as a product of a common factor and a sum. Example: 6x + 18 factors to 6(x + 3). Factoring is the reverse of expanding.
- **Greatest Common Factor (GCF)** -- the largest factor shared by all terms in an expression.

### Instruction Notes

**Step 1 -- Distribute with a positive factor.**

Say: "When we have a number multiplied by a sum or difference in parentheses, we distribute -- we multiply the outside number by each term inside."

Write: 4(x + 3)

Say: "Multiply 4 by x, then multiply 4 by 3."
- 4(x + 3) = 4 · x + 4 · 3 = 4x + 12

Draw arrows from the 4 to each term inside the parentheses.

Say: "Let me verify. If x = 5: 4(5 + 3) = 4(8) = 32. And 4(5) + 12 = 20 + 12 = 32. They match."

**Step 2 -- Distribute with a negative factor.**

Say: "This is where students often make mistakes. When the number outside is negative, you must multiply every term by that negative number."

Write: -3(2x + 5)

Say: "Multiply -3 by 2x, then multiply -3 by 5."
- -3(2x + 5) = (-3)(2x) + (-3)(5) = -6x + (-15) = -6x - 15

Say: "Be very careful with signs. Negative times positive gives negative for both terms."

Write: -2(x - 7)

Say: "Multiply -2 by x, then multiply -2 by (-7)."
- -2(x - 7) = (-2)(x) + (-2)(-7) = -2x + 14

Say: "Negative times negative gives positive, so -2 times -7 is +14. This is a common place for sign errors."

**Step 3 -- Factoring as the reverse of distributing.**

Say: "Factoring is the reverse of expanding. Instead of removing parentheses, we create them by pulling out a common factor."

Write: 10x + 15

Say: "What number divides evenly into both 10 and 15? The GCF is 5."
- 10x + 15 = 5(2x + 3)

Check: 5(2x + 3) = 10x + 15. Correct.

Write: 8y - 12

Say: "The GCF of 8 and 12 is 4."
- 8y - 12 = 4(2y - 3)

Check: 4(2y - 3) = 8y - 12. Correct.

### Worked Examples

**Example A: Positive factor, subtraction inside**
Expand: 5(3y - 2)

5(3y) - 5(2) = 15y - 10

Check with y = 4: 5(3(4) - 2) = 5(12 - 2) = 5(10) = 50. And 15(4) - 10 = 60 - 10 = 50.

Answer: **15y - 10**

**Example B: Negative factor**
Expand: -4(x + 6)

(-4)(x) + (-4)(6) = -4x + (-24) = -4x - 24

Check with x = 3: -4(3 + 6) = -4(9) = -36. And -4(3) - 24 = -12 - 24 = -36.

Answer: **-4x - 24**

**Example C: Factoring**
Factor: 12a + 18

GCF of 12 and 18 is 6.
12a / 6 = 2a. 18 / 6 = 3.
12a + 18 = 6(2a + 3)

Check: 6(2a + 3) = 12a + 18. Correct.

Answer: **6(2a + 3)**

## Guided Practice (10 minutes)

1. Expand: 7(x + 4)
   - 7x + 28
   - **Facilitator tip:** "Multiply 7 by x, then 7 by 4."

2. Expand: 3(2a - 5)
   - 6a - 15
   - **Facilitator tip:** "3 times 2a is 6a. 3 times -5 is -15."

3. Expand: -5(y + 3)
   - -5y - 15
   - **Facilitator tip:** "Negative 5 times y is -5y. Negative 5 times 3 is -15."

4. Expand: -2(4x - 9)
   - -8x + 18
   - **Facilitator tip:** "Negative times negative 9 gives positive 18. Watch the signs."

5. Factor: 6x + 9
   - GCF is 3. 6x + 9 = **3(2x + 3)**
   - **Facilitator tip:** "What is the largest number that divides both 6 and 9?"

6. Factor: 15y - 20
   - GCF is 5. 15y - 20 = **5(3y - 4)**
   - **Facilitator tip:** "Divide each term by 5 and write what remains inside parentheses."

## Independent Practice (10-15 minutes)

### Part A: Expanding Expressions

1. Expand: 6(x + 5)
2. Expand: 4(3y - 1)
3. Expand: -3(a + 8)
4. Expand: -7(2b - 3)
5. Expand: 9(x + y)
6. Expand: -1(5x - 4)
7. Expand: 2(3x + 4y - 5)
8. Expand: -6(2a - b + 3)

### Part B: Factoring Expressions

9. Factor: 8x + 12
10. Factor: 14y - 21
11. Factor: 10a + 25
12. Factor: 3x + 3

### Part C: Word Problems

13. A rectangular garden has a length of x feet and a width of (x + 4) feet. The area is given by x(x + 4). Expand this expression.
14. Tickets to a school play cost $5 each. A group buys (n + 3) tickets. Write and expand an expression for the total cost.

### Answer Key

**Part A**
1. 6(x + 5) = **6x + 30**
2. 4(3y - 1) = **12y - 4**
3. -3(a + 8) = **-3a - 24**
4. -7(2b - 3) = **-14b + 21**
5. 9(x + y) = **9x + 9y**
6. -1(5x - 4) = **-5x + 4**
7. 2(3x + 4y - 5) = **6x + 8y - 10**
8. -6(2a - b + 3) = **-12a + 6b - 18**

**Part B**
9. GCF = 4. 8x + 12 = **4(2x + 3)**
10. GCF = 7. 14y - 21 = **7(2y - 3)**
11. GCF = 5. 10a + 25 = **5(2a + 5)**
12. GCF = 3. 3x + 3 = **3(x + 1)**

**Part C**
13. x(x + 4) = **x^2 + 4x**
14. Total cost = 5(n + 3) = **5n + 15** dollars

## Assessment Check

### Exit Ticket
**Problem 1:** Expand: -4(3x - 5)

**Problem 2:** Factor: 16y + 24

**Target response:**
1. -4(3x - 5) = -12x + 20
2. GCF = 8. 16y + 24 = 8(2y + 3)

**Scoring:**
- 2 out of 2 correct = Mastery demonstrated.
- 1 out of 2 correct = Partial understanding; identify whether the gap is in expanding or factoring.
- 0 out of 2 correct = Reteach needed; return to the distributive property with numbers before variables.

## Extension
- **Three-Term Distribution:** Expand -3(2x - 4y + 7). (Answer: -6x + 12y - 21)
- **Reverse Engineering:** An expression expands to 10x - 35. What was the factored form? (Answer: 5(2x - 7))
