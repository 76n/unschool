# Math — Grade 2 — Unit 2, Lesson 8

## Topic
Speed and Accuracy: Fluency with All Facts Within 20

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this lesson, the student will be able to:
- Recall addition facts with sums of 16-20 within 3 seconds each
- Recall the related subtraction facts within 3 seconds each
- Demonstrate fluency across ALL addition and subtraction facts within 20

## Prerequisites
- Lessons 1-5: All strategies (counting on, doubles, near-doubles, make 10, think-addition)
- Lesson 6: Fluency with sums to 10
- Lesson 7: Fluency with sums to 15

## Materials Needed
- Complete set of flashcards for all addition/subtraction facts within 20
- Timer or stopwatch
- Paper and pencil
- "I Know It," "Almost," and "Still Learning" envelopes
- Number line 0-20

**Standards:** 2.OA.2

---

## Warm-Up (5 minutes)
**Purpose:** Review sums-to-15 facts and warm up for larger sums.

**Mixed Review — Oral (2 minutes):**
1. 7 + 6 = ___
2. 14 - 8 = ___

**Bridge to Sums 16-20 (3 minutes):**
3. 8 + 8 = ___
4. 9 + 9 = ___

Ask: "What strategies will help with facts that sum to 16, 17, 18, 19, or 20?"

**Facilitator note:** The student should mention doubles (8+8, 9+9), near-doubles (8+9), and make-10 (9+7, 9+8, 8+7). If they do not mention these, prompt: "What if one number is 8 or 9?"

---

## Direct Instruction (10 minutes)

### Key Vocabulary
- **Full fluency:** Knowing all basic facts within 20 automatically — both addition and subtraction

### Instruction Notes

**Step 1: The Final Group of Facts (3 minutes)**

Say: "This is the last group of facts to learn! Facts with sums from 16 to 20."

Write them out:

| Sum = 16 | Sum = 17 | Sum = 18 | Sum = 19 | Sum = 20 |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| 7 + 9 | 8 + 9 | 9 + 9 | — | — |
| 8 + 8 | 9 + 8 | — | — | — |
| 9 + 7 | — | — | — | — |

Say: "Look how few facts are in this group! There are only really 4 unique facts: 7+9=16, 8+8=16, 8+9=17, and 9+9=18. Facts like 9+10=19 and 10+10=20 are easy because anything plus 10 just changes the tens digit."

**Note:** For the standard set of basic facts (addends 0-10), we also have:
- 10 + 6 = 16, 10 + 7 = 17, 10 + 8 = 18, 10 + 9 = 19, 10 + 10 = 20

Say: "Adding 10 is a freebie — you already know that from place value!"

**Step 2: Strategies for the Hard Four (5 minutes)**

**7 + 9 = 16**
- Make-10: 9 + 1 + 6 = 16 (take 1 from 7, give it to 9)
- Alternative: 7 + 10 - 1 = 16 (add 10, subtract 1)
- Say the fact 3 times together: "7 + 9 = 16, 7 + 9 = 16, 7 + 9 = 16."

**8 + 8 = 16**
- Doubles! "Two octopuses — 16 arms."
- This should already be automatic from Lesson 2.

**8 + 9 = 17**
- Make-10: 9 + 1 + 7 = 17
- Near-doubles: 8 + 8 + 1 = 17 (or 9 + 9 - 1 = 17)
- Say it 3 times: "8 + 9 = 17, 8 + 9 = 17, 8 + 9 = 17."

**9 + 9 = 18**
- Doubles! "Two nines — 18."
- Should be automatic from Lesson 2.

**Step 3: Subtraction Connections (2 minutes)**

Say: "Now let's get the subtraction facts for free using fact families."
- 16 - 7 = 9, 16 - 9 = 7
- 16 - 8 = 8
- 17 - 8 = 9, 17 - 9 = 8
- 18 - 9 = 9

Say: "If you know 8 + 9 = 17, you instantly know 17 - 8 = 9 and 17 - 9 = 8!"

### Worked Examples

**Example A:** 9 + 7 = ?
- Make-10: "9 needs 1 to make 10. Take 1 from 7, leaving 6. 10 + 6 = 16."
- Answer: 16.
- Subtraction connection: 16 - 9 = 7 and 16 - 7 = 9.

**Example B:** 17 - 8 = ?
- Think-addition: "8 + ? = 17. I know 8 + 9 = 17. So 17 - 8 = 9."
- Answer: 9.

## Guided Practice (10 minutes)

**Part A — The Final Four (5 minutes):**

Drill the four key facts and their subtraction partners:

1. 7 + 9 = ___
   *Ask: "What is 16 - 9? How about 16 - 7?"*
   (16; 7; 9)

2. 8 + 9 = ___
   *Ask: "What strategy did you use? Now: 17 - 9 = ?"*
   (17; 8)

3. 9 + 9 = ___
   *Ask: "What is 18 - 9?"*
   (18; 9)

4. 8 + 8 = ___
   *Ask: "What is 16 - 8?"*
   (16; 8)

**Part B — Full Range Mixed Drill (5 minutes):**

Mix facts from ALL ranges (sums to 10, to 15, and to 20). Call out 10 facts — student answers orally. Time the entire set.

5. 6 + 5 = ___ (11)
6. 9 + 7 = ___ (16)
7. 13 - 6 = ___ (7)
8. 8 + 4 = ___ (12)
9. 17 - 8 = ___ (9)
10. 5 + 9 = ___ (14)

Record the total time. This is the student's benchmark.

## Check for Understanding (5 minutes)

### Exit Ticket
Solve — aim for under 3 seconds each:
1. 9 + 7 = ___
2. 17 - 8 = ___
3. 8 + 9 = ___
4. 16 - 9 = ___

**Scoring:**
- 4/4 correct AND all within 3 seconds = Mastery
- 4/4 correct but some slow = Approaching mastery
- 0-3/4 correct = Needs reteaching

### If Mastery Is Not Met
- With only 4 key facts in this range, targeted practice is very focused. Write each troublesome fact on a sticky note and post it somewhere visible (refrigerator, bathroom mirror). The student reads it every time they pass.
- Do 3 minutes of oral drill on these specific facts at the start of each day.
- This lesson can be repeated before moving on. Fluency within 20 is the major outcome of this unit.

### If Mastery Is Met
Say: "You now know ALL your facts within 20! That is incredible. In the next lesson, we will play some fun math games to keep your skills sharp and make sure you do not forget any of these facts."

## Extension (Optional)

**Grand Tour Drill:** Write all facts within 20 on a sheet (or use the full flashcard deck). Time the student going through the entire set. Record the total time and number correct. This becomes their baseline for Lesson 10.

**Fact Families Poster:** Have the student choose their 3 favorite fact families and create a poster for each one — illustrating all 4 facts in the family with drawings, colors, and real-world examples.

## Teacher/Facilitator Notes

### Common Errors
1. **7 + 9 = 15 (off by 1):** Student uses make-10 but loses track: 9 + 1 + 5 instead of 9 + 1 + 6. Fix: "You took 1 from the 7. What is left? 7 - 1 = 6, not 5."
2. **Confusing 16 and 17:** Student says 8 + 9 = 16 or 7 + 9 = 17. Fix: "8 + 8 = 16, and 8 + 9 is one MORE, so 17. 7 + 9 is one LESS than 8 + 9, so 16."
3. **Subtraction of near-20 facts is slow:** Think-addition takes effort for 17 - 8 because the student does not have 8 + 9 = 17 automated yet. Fix: The subtraction will get faster as the addition gets faster. Focus on the addition fact first.
4. **Regression on easier facts:** Student is so focused on the new facts that previously fluent facts (sums to 10) slow down. Fix: Always include a mix of easy and hard facts in practice. This is why the mixed drill is important.

### Pacing Advice
- This lesson may need 2-3 sessions if the student is also still working on sums to 15. That is perfectly fine — mastery matters more than speed through the curriculum.
- The full-range mixed drill in Guided Practice is the most important activity. It tells you whether the student can handle ALL facts, not just the new ones.
- Target total time: 35-40 minutes.
