# Unit Assessment — Grade 2 Math, Unit 2: Fluency Within 20

**Grade:** 2
**Unit:** 2 — Fluency Within 20
**Assessment Type:** Summative (unit-level)
**Administration:** Written (student completes independently)
**Estimated Duration:** 15-20 minutes (Part A: 3 minutes timed; Part B: untimed, approximately 12-17 minutes)
**Materials:** Paper, pencil, timer or stopwatch

---

## Assessment Overview

This assessment has two parts:

| Part | What It Measures | Format | Items | Time |
|------|-----------------|--------|-------|------|
| A | Speed and accuracy | Timed: 3 minutes | 20 mixed facts | 3 minutes |
| B | Strategy understanding and application | Untimed | 10 items | ~12-17 minutes |
| — | **Total** | — | **30 items** | ~15-20 minutes |

**Mastery threshold:** 90% overall (27 of 30 correct) AND at least 16 of 20 correct on Part A (speed section) within the 3-minute time limit.

---

## Administration Instructions

- **Part A:** Set a timer for 3 minutes. Say: "Solve as many as you can. Write only the answer. Skip any you are unsure of and come back to them. When the timer goes off, stop."
- **Part B:** No timer. The student may take as long as needed. Read the word problem (B8) aloud if the student requests.
- **No manipulatives** for Part A (this tests recall, not strategy use). Ten-frames or number lines may be available for Part B if the student needs them.

*Scoring notes for Part B:*
- B1: 1 point for all 4 facts correct. 0 points if any fact is missing or wrong.
- B5: 1 point if the answer AND the explanation are both correct.
- B6: 1 point if all blanks are filled correctly.
- B7: 1 point if at least 3 of 4 strategies are reasonable choices.
- B8: 1 point for correct answer with a valid number sentence.
- B9: 1 point for "True" with a reasonable explanation referencing fact families or inverse operations.
- All other items: 1 point for the correct answer.

## Rubric and Mastery Determination

| Level | Score | Part A Speed | Meaning | Next Steps |
|-------|-------|-------------|---------|------------|
| **Mastered** | 27-30 (90-100%) | 16+ correct in 3 min | Fluent with facts within 20 | Advance to Unit 3. Include fact review in daily warm-ups. |
| **Approaching** | 23-26 (77-89%) | 12-15 correct in 3 min | Most facts known; some gaps | Identify weak facts using the analysis below. Daily flashcard practice for 2 weeks, then reassess. |
| **Developing** | Below 23 (<77%) | Below 12 in 3 min | Significant gaps in fluency | Return to Lessons 6-8. Determine whether the issue is strategy understanding or automaticity. Reteach as needed. |

### Part B: Strategy and Application

**B1.** Fact family for 7, 8, 15:
- 7 + 8 = 15
- 8 + 7 = 15
- 15 - 7 = 8
- 15 - 8 = 7

**B2.** 7 (6 + 7 = 13)

**B3.** 7 (7 + 9 = 16)

**B4.** 14 - 6 = 8. Addition fact: 6 + 8 = 14.

**B5.** 7 + 8 = 15. Doubles fact: 7 + 7 = 14, plus 1 more = 15 (or 8 + 8 = 16, minus 1 = 15).

**B6.** Make-10 steps for 9 + 6:
- 9 + 1 = 10
- 6 - 1 = 5
- 10 + 5 = 15
- So 9 + 6 = 15

**B7.** Reasonable strategy choices:

| Fact | Acceptable Strategies |
|------|----------------------|
| 8 + 3 | CO (counting on) or M10 |
| 5 + 5 | D (doubles) |
| 9 + 4 | M10 (make-10) |
| 6 + 7 | ND (near-doubles) or M10 |

Note: Accept any reasonable strategy choice. The point is that the student can identify an efficient strategy, not that they pick the "right" one.

**B8.** Number sentence: 16 - 9 = 7 (or equivalently, 9 + ? = 16).
Answer: 7 toy cars.

**B9.** True. Explanation should reference fact families or the idea that addition and subtraction are related/inverse operations. Sample: "They are in the same fact family. If 9 + 8 = 17, then taking 8 away from 17 gives back the 9."

**B10.** 15 (because 15 - 7 = 8; equivalently, 7 + 8 = 15).
