# Math — Grade 2 — Unit 3, Lesson 8

## Topic
Multiplying with 2s and 5s

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this lesson, the student will be able to:
- Solve ×2 multiplication problems fluently (using skip counting or doubles)
- Solve ×5 multiplication problems fluently (using skip counting)
- Connect ×2 to doubles facts from Unit 1/2

## Prerequisites
- Multiplication as skip counting (Lesson 7)
- Skip counting by 2s and 5s (Lesson 3)
- Doubles facts (Grade 1 Unit 1 / Grade 2 Unit 2)

## Materials Needed
- Number line
- Hundred chart
- Paper and pencil

---

## Warm-Up (5 minutes)
**Purpose:** Activate doubles and skip counting.

1. "4 + 4 = ?" (8) "That's a double! And it's also 2 × 4. Two groups of 4."
2. "7 + 7 = ?" (14) "That's 2 × 7."
3. "Count by 5s to 50." (5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50)

"Today you'll become a master of ×2 and ×5 facts!"

---

## Direct Instruction (10 minutes)
**Purpose:** Build ×2 and ×5 fact fluency through patterns.

### Instruction Notes

**×2 facts = Doubles.**
"Multiplying by 2 means 2 groups of a number. That's the same as adding a number to itself — a double!"

| Multiplication | Double | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 2 × 1 | 1 + 1 | 2 |
| 2 × 2 | 2 + 2 | 4 |
| 2 × 3 | 3 + 3 | 6 |
| 2 × 4 | 4 + 4 | 8 |
| 2 × 5 | 5 + 5 | 10 |
| 2 × 6 | 6 + 6 | 12 |
| 2 × 7 | 7 + 7 | 14 |
| 2 × 8 | 8 + 8 | 16 |
| 2 × 9 | 9 + 9 | 18 |
| 2 × 10 | 10 + 10 | 20 |

"You already know your doubles from addition. So you already know your ×2 facts!"

**×5 facts = Skip counting by 5s.**
"Multiplying by 5 means counting by 5s."

| Multiplication | Skip Count | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 × 5 | 5 | 5 |
| 2 × 5 | 5, 10 | 10 |
| 3 × 5 | 5, 10, 15 | 15 |
| 4 × 5 | 5, 10, 15, 20 | 20 |
| 5 × 5 | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 | 25 |

"Notice: ×5 answers always end in 0 or 5."

**Pattern discovery:**
"Look at the ×5 answers: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50. Can you see a pattern?" (They alternate: 5-0-5-0-5-0...)

---

## Guided Practice (10 minutes)
**Purpose:** Rapid practice with ×2 and ×5.

**Activity 1: ×2 speed round**
Call out problems, student answers:
- 2 × 3 = ? (6) "Double 3!"
- 2 × 8 = ? (16) "Double 8!"
- 2 × 5 = ? (10)
- 2 × 9 = ? (18)
- 2 × 6 = ? (12)

**Activity 2: ×5 speed round**
- 3 × 5 = ? (15)
- 6 × 5 = ? (30)
- 8 × 5 = ? (40)
- 4 × 5 = ? (20)
- 9 × 5 = ? (45)

**Activity 3: Mixed**
- 7 × 2 = ? (14)
- 7 × 5 = ? (35)
- "Which was easier to figure out? Why?" (×2 uses doubles; ×5 uses skip counting)

---

## Independent Practice (10 minutes)
**Purpose:** Build fluency with ×2 and ×5 facts.

**★ On-level:**
1. 2 × 4 = ___ (8)
2. 2 × 7 = ___ (14)
3. 2 × 9 = ___ (18)
4. 5 × 3 = ___ (15)
5. 5 × 6 = ___ (30)
6. 5 × 8 = ___ (40)

**★★ Stretch:**
7. Fill in: ___ × 2 = 16 (8)
8. Fill in: ___ × 5 = 45 (9)
9. "Each hand has 5 fingers. There are 6 people. How many fingers altogether?" (6 × 5 = 30, then × 2 for both hands = 60. Or: each person has 10 fingers, 6 × 10 = 60.)

**★★★ Challenge:**
10. "Is 2 × 8 the same as 8 × 2? Show with skip counting." (2×8: count by 8 twice = 8, 16. 8×2: count by 2 eight times = 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16. Both = 16!)
11. "What is 10 × 5?" (50) "What is 10 × 2?" (20) "Why is multiplying by 10 easy?" (Just add a 0!)

### Interleaved Review
- "Draw a 3 × 4 array. How many total?" (12) *(Lesson 4)*
- "78 − 9 = ___" (69)

---

## Check for Understanding (5 minutes)
**Purpose:** Confirm ×2 and ×5 fluency.

**Exit Ticket:**

1. 2 × 6 = ___ (12)
2. 7 × 5 = ___ (35)

**Mastery = Both correct within a few seconds (using doubles or skip counting, not counting by ones).**

### If Mastery Is Not Met
- ×2: Practice doubles daily. "I say a number, you double it." Flash-card style.
- ×5: Practice skip counting by 5s daily. Use fingers: hold up one finger per count (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 — that's 5 fingers up for 5 × 5 = 25).

### If Mastery Is Met
Move to Lesson 9 (Multiplying with 10s).

---

## Teacher/Facilitator Notes

**×2 and ×5 are the easiest multiplication facts** because students already have the underlying skills (doubles and skip counting by 5s). Building confidence with these "easy" facts motivates the harder facts in Grade 3.

**Finger trick for ×5:** Hold up fingers one at a time, counting by 5s. The number of fingers up × 5 = the count. 7 fingers up (hold up 2 more after running out of one hand) = 35.

**Connection to money:** ×5 facts correspond to nickel counting (5¢, 10¢, 15¢...). ×2 facts are like counting pairs. Real-world connections help.
