# Unit 6 Assessment — Answer Key
## For Facilitator Use Only

---

### Scoring Guide

**Part A–D (12 items):** 1 point each = 12 points
**Performance Tasks:** Holistic scoring (see rubric below)
**Mastery threshold:** 10/12 or higher on Parts A–D, plus Satisfactory on both Performance Tasks

---

### Part A: Measurement

**Item 1:** 5 inches

**Item 2:** 8 cm

**Item 3:**
- a. The blue ribbon (14 in.) is longer.
- b. 14 − 9 = 5 inches longer

**Item 4:**
27 − 12 = 15 cm
Award 1 point for correct equation and correct answer.

---

### Part B: Time

**Item 5:** 3:45
*(Long hand on 9 = count by 5s: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45 → 45 minutes; short hand past 3 → hour is 3)*

**Item 6:** Clock face shows:
- Short hand: pointing just past 7 (between 7 and 8, closer to 7)
- Long hand: pointing at 5
Accept drawings with short hand clearly past 7 and long hand clearly on 5.

---

### Part C: Money

**Item 7:**
Quarter: 25. Dimes: 35, 45. Pennies: 46, 47, 48.
Total: **48¢**

**Item 8:**
- a. 35 + 20 = 55¢
- b. 3 dimes + 3 nickels: 10 + 10 + 10 + 5 + 5 + 5 = **45¢**
- c. 45¢ < 55¢ → **NO**, not enough

Award 1 point total for item 8 only if all three parts are correct. Or award partial credit (a–c separately) per facilitator discretion.

**Item 9:**
35 − 32 = **3¢** change

**Answer Key:**
- a. Ribbon C (25 cm)
- b. Ribbon B (11 cm)
- c. 25 − 11 = 14 cm longer

### Common Error Patterns and Reteach Guidance

| Error Pattern | Likely Cause | Reteach |
|---|---|---|
| Off-by-one on ruler | Starting at "1" not "0" | Return to Lesson 1 concrete phase |
| Wrong time on clock | Confusing hour/minute hands | Lesson 5; drill hand ID before time reading |
| Incorrect coin total | Not sorting; wrong coin values | Lesson 7; rebuild with sorting mat |
| Bar misread | Not reading axis scale | Lesson 9; review scale language |
| Line plot errors | Not counting data points correctly | Lesson 10; sort data into groups first |
| Performance task computation | Multi-step confusion | Use graphic organizer; identify each step separately |
