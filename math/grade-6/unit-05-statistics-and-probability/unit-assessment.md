(c) Mode = **12 years** (appears 3 times) *(1 point)*

The mean (12.6) is close but is pulled slightly up by the 15-year-old. **Item 6:** *(6 points)*

(c) Range = 680−480 = **200 calories** *(1 point)*

    Upper half (last 5): 515, 520, 525, 530, 680 → Q3 = **525**
