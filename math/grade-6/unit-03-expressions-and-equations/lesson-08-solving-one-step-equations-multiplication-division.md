# Math — Grade 6 — Unit 3, Lesson 8

## Topic
Solving One-Step Equations: Multiplication and Division

## Learning Objectives
- Solve equations of the form ax = b by dividing both sides by the coefficient.
- Solve equations of the form x/a = b by multiplying both sides by the divisor.
- Recognize that multiplication and division are inverse operations.
- Check solutions by substituting back into the original equation.

## Prerequisites
- Solving addition and subtraction equations using inverse operations (Lesson 7).
- Understanding of the balance scale metaphor (Lessons 6-7).
- Fluency with multiplication and division of whole numbers, fractions, and decimals.

## Materials Needed
- Whiteboard and markers (or projector).
- Balance scale diagram (for continued visual support).
- Student worksheet (Guided Practice and Independent Practice problems).
- Paper and pencil.

**Standards:** 6.EE.7 — Solve real-world and mathematical problems by writing and solving equations of the form x + p = q and x - p = q for cases in which p, q, and x are all nonnegative rational numbers. (Extended here to px = q and x/p = q.)

## Warm-Up (5 minutes)

**Purpose:** Connect multiplication/division equations to prior experience and build toward the inverse operation approach.

1. Write on the board: 3 x ? = 18. Ask: "What number goes in the box?" (6.) "How did you figure it out?" (Most will say "I divided 18 by 3.")
2. Write: ? ÷ 4 = 5. Ask: "What goes in the box?" (20.) "How did you figure it out?" (Most will say "I multiplied 5 by 4.")
3. Say: "In Lesson 7, we learned that addition and subtraction are inverse operations — they undo each other. The same is true for multiplication and division. If a variable is being multiplied by a number, we divide to undo it. If a variable is being divided by a number, we multiply to undo it. Today we solve equations using these ideas."

## Direct Instruction (10-15 minutes)

### Key Vocabulary
- **Coefficient** — The number multiplied by the variable. In 3x, the coefficient is 3.
- **Inverse operations** — Operations that undo each other. Multiplication and division are inverse operations.
- **Isolate the variable** — Get the variable alone on one side of the equation by undoing operations.

### Instruction Notes

**Step 1 — Solving ax = b (multiplication equation).**

Write on the board: 4x = 24.

Draw the balance scale: left side has "4x" (four groups of x), right side has "24."

Say: "4x means 4 times x. To undo multiplying by 4, we divide by 4. And we must do the same to both sides."

Write the solution steps:

```
4x = 24
4x ÷ 4 = 24 ÷ 4    (divide both sides by 4)
x = 6
```

Say: "On the left side, 4 divided by 4 is 1, so we are left with 1x, which is just x. On the right side, 24 ÷ 4 = 6."

Say: "Check: 4(6) = 24. 24 = 24. Correct."

Say: "You can also think of it on the balance scale: if 4 groups of x weigh 24, then each group weighs 24 ÷ 4 = 6."

**Step 2 — Solving x/a = b (division equation).**

Write on the board: x/3 = 7.

Say: "Here x is being divided by 3. To undo division, we multiply. Multiply both sides by 3."

```
x/3 = 7
(x/3) x 3 = 7 x 3    (multiply both sides by 3)
x = 21
```

Say: "On the left, dividing by 3 then multiplying by 3 cancels out, leaving x. On the right, 7 x 3 = 21."

Say: "Check: 21/3 = 7. 7 = 7. Correct."

**Step 3 — Connecting to the balance metaphor.**

Say: "Think of x/3 = 7 this way: x was split into 3 equal parts, and each part is 7. To find the whole amount x, multiply 7 by 3 to get 21. The balance scale helps: if one-third of x weighs 7, then the whole x weighs 3 times as much."

**Step 4 — Equations with decimals and fractions.**

Write: 5x = 12.5.

```
5x = 12.5
5x ÷ 5 = 12.5 ÷ 5
x = 2.5
```

Say: "Check: 5(2.5) = 12.5. Correct."

Write: n/2 = 3.5.

```
n/2 = 3.5
n/2 x 2 = 3.5 x 2
n = 7
```

Say: "Check: 7/2 = 3.5. Correct."

Write: (1/3)x = 9.

Say: "This says one-third of x equals 9. To undo multiplying by 1/3, we multiply by the reciprocal, which is 3."

```
(1/3)x = 9
(1/3)x x 3 = 9 x 3
x = 27
```

Say: "Check: (1/3)(27) = 9. Correct."

### Worked Examples

**Example A: Multiplication equation with whole numbers.**

Solve: 7n = 56.

- Divide both sides by 7: n = 56 ÷ 7 = 8.
- Check: 7(8) = 56. 56 = 56. Correct.
- Answer: **n = 8**.

**Example B: Division equation.**

Solve: y/6 = 9.

- Multiply both sides by 6: y = 9 x 6 = 54.
- Check: 54/6 = 9. 9 = 9. Correct.
- Answer: **y = 54**.

**Example C: Multiplication equation with a decimal result.**

Solve: 8x = 20.

- Divide both sides by 8: x = 20 ÷ 8 = 2.5.
- Check: 8(2.5) = 20. 20 = 20. Correct.
- Answer: **x = 2.5**.

## Guided Practice (10 minutes)

Solve each equation. Show your steps and check your answer.

1. 6x = 42
   - Answer: x = 42 ÷ 6 = **7**. Check: 6(7) = 42.

2. n/5 = 8
   - Answer: n = 8 x 5 = **40**. Check: 40/5 = 8.

3. 9y = 63
   - Answer: y = 63 ÷ 9 = **7**. Check: 9(7) = 63.

4. m/4 = 3.5
   - Answer: m = 3.5 x 4 = **14**. Check: 14/4 = 3.5.

5. 3x = 7.5
   - Answer: x = 7.5 ÷ 3 = **2.5**. Check: 3(2.5) = 7.5.

6. (1/2)n = 10
   - Answer: n = 10 x 2 = **20**. Check: (1/2)(20) = 10.

**Facilitator tip:** For problem 6, if the student is unsure how to "undo" multiplying by 1/2, explain: "Multiplying by 1/2 is the same as dividing by 2. To undo dividing by 2, multiply by 2." Alternatively: "Multiply both sides by the reciprocal of 1/2, which is 2."

### Part C: Word Problems

11. A pack of 6 identical notebooks costs $13.50. Write and solve an equation to find the cost of one notebook.

12. A student reads the same number of pages each day. After 5 days, she has read 80 pages total. Write and solve an equation to find how many pages she reads per day.

13. If you divide your age by 4, you get 3. Write and solve an equation to find your age.

14. A rectangle has an area of 48 square centimeters and a length of 8 cm. The formula for area is A = lw. Write and solve an equation to find the width.

### Answer Key

**Part A**

1. 5x = 35. Divide by 5: x = 35 ÷ 5 = **7**. Check: 5(7) = 35.
2. 8n = 72. Divide by 8: n = 72 ÷ 8 = **9**. Check: 8(9) = 72.
3. 12y = 60. Divide by 12: y = 60 ÷ 12 = **5**. Check: 12(5) = 60.
4. 3m = 16.5. Divide by 3: m = 16.5 ÷ 3 = **5.5**. Check: 3(5.5) = 16.5.
5. 10p = 45. Divide by 10: p = 45 ÷ 10 = **4.5**. Check: 10(4.5) = 45.

**Part B**

6. x/7 = 4. Multiply by 7: x = 4 x 7 = **28**. Check: 28/7 = 4.
7. n/3 = 15. Multiply by 3: n = 15 x 3 = **45**. Check: 45/3 = 15.
8. y/10 = 2.5. Multiply by 10: y = 2.5 x 10 = **25**. Check: 25/10 = 2.5.
9. m/6 = 9. Multiply by 6: m = 9 x 6 = **54**. Check: 54/6 = 9.
10. a/2 = 18. Multiply by 2: a = 18 x 2 = **36**. Check: 36/2 = 18.

**Part C**

11. Let c = cost of one notebook. 6c = 13.50. Divide by 6: c = 13.50 ÷ 6 = **$2.25**. One notebook costs $2.25.
12. Let p = pages per day. 5p = 80. Divide by 5: p = 80 ÷ 5 = **16 pages**. She reads 16 pages per day.
13. Let a = your age. a/4 = 3. Multiply by 4: a = 3 x 4 = **12**. You are 12 years old.
14. Area = length x width: 48 = 8w. Divide by 8: w = 48 ÷ 8 = **6 cm**. The width is 6 cm.

## Assessment Check

Solve: 9x = 54. Show your steps and check.

**Target response:** Divide both sides by 9: x = 54 ÷ 9 = **6**. Check: 9(6) = 54. 54 = 54. Correct. If the student subtracts 9 from both sides instead of dividing, explain: "The equation says 9 times x, not 9 plus x. To undo multiplication, we divide."

## Extension

- **Mixed Equation Practice:** Without being told the operation, solve these equations by identifying the operation and applying the correct inverse: (a) x + 11 = 30, (b) 7x = 49, (c) x - 5 = 22, (d) x/8 = 3. Write a sentence explaining which inverse operation you used for each.

- **Equation Families:** The equations 3x = 12, x/4 = 1, x + 8 = 12, and x - 2 = 2 all have the same solution. Find the solution (x = 4) and then create a new "equation family" — four equations (one for each operation) that all have the solution x = 9.
