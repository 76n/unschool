# Unit Assessment — Grade 3 Math, Unit 2: Multiplication Facts (3-9)

**Grade:** 3
**Unit:** 2 — Multiplication Facts (3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9)
**Assessment Type:** Summative (unit-level)
**Administration:** Written; facilitator reads word problems aloud (Section D)
**Estimated Duration:** 25-35 minutes
**Materials:** Paper and pencil. No manipulatives, charts, or calculators.

---

## Assessment Overview

| Section | Skill Assessed | Lesson Source | Items |
|---------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| A | Fact fluency — new fact families (×3, ×4, ×6, ×7, ×8, ×9) | Lessons 1-6 | 8 |
| B | Strategy knowledge | Lessons 1-6, 8 | 3 |
| C | Square numbers and patterns | Lesson 7 | 3 |
| D | Word problems (one-step and two-step) | Lesson 10 | 4 |
| E | Cumulative review (Unit 1 facts) | Unit 1 | 2 |
| — | **Total** | — | **20** |

**Mastery threshold:** 90% (18 of 20 correct) AND at least 50% in each section.

---

## Administration Instructions

- Students write answers and show work where requested.
- Section A is a timed fluency section: 4 minutes maximum. Encourage the student to work quickly but carefully.
- Sections B-E are untimed. Read Section D word problems aloud.
- No multiplication charts, finger-counting for ×9, or other aids during Section A. Sections B-E allow the student to use strategies (but not reference charts).

## Section B: Strategy Knowledge (3 items)

**B1.** "A student forgot what 4 × 7 equals. Describe the 'double the double' strategy to help them figure it out. Show the steps."

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Describes two doubling steps: double 7 = 14, double 14 = 28. Answer: 28. | 1 point |
| Partially correct (describes only one step, or correct answer without strategy explanation) | 0.5 points |

**B2.** "Use the '×10 minus one group' strategy to solve 9 × 8. Show your work."

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Shows: 10 × 8 = 80, then 80 - 8 = 72. Answer: 72. | 1 point |
| Correct answer but no strategy work shown | 0.5 points |

**B3.** "Solve 6 × 7 using the '×5 plus one more group' strategy OR the 'double ×3' strategy. Show which one you chose and the steps."

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| ×5 plus one more: 5 × 7 = 35, + 7 = 42. Answer: 42. | 1 point |
| OR double ×3: 3 × 7 = 21, double = 42. Answer: 42. | 1 point |
| Correct answer but no strategy work shown | 0.5 points |

**C1.** "List all the square numbers from 1 to 50."

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49 (all seven, no extras) | 1 point |
| 5-6 correct with no incorrect numbers | 0.5 points |

**C2.** "Is 48 a square number? Explain how you know."

| Response | Score |

## Section D: Word Problems (4 items)

*Read each problem aloud. Student writes the equation and answer.*

**D1.** "A spider has 8 legs. How many legs do 7 spiders have?"

Equation: _______________
Answer: ___ legs

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| 8 × 7 = 56 (or 7 × 8 = 56). Answer: 56 legs. | 1 point |
| Correct answer with missing/wrong equation | 0.5 points |

**D2.** "There are 9 rows of desks in a classroom. Each row has 6 desks. How many desks are there?"

Equation: _______________
Answer: ___ desks

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| 9 × 6 = 54. Answer: 54 desks. | 1 point |
| Correct answer with missing/wrong equation | 0.5 points |

**D3.** (Two-step) "A baker puts 6 muffins on each tray. She fills 8 trays. Then she eats 3 muffins. How many muffins are left?"

Equation/Steps: _______________
Answer: ___ muffins

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Step 1: 6 × 8 = 48 (or 8 × 6 = 48). Step 2: 48 - 3 = 45. Answer: 45 muffins. | 1 point |
| Correct multiplication but arithmetic error in subtraction (e.g., 48 - 3 = 44) | 0.5 points |
| Correct answer with no work shown | 0.5 points |

**D4.** (Two-step) "Carlos has 10 stickers. He buys 4 packs with 7 stickers in each pack. How many stickers does he have now?"

Equation/Steps: _______________
Answer: ___ stickers

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Step 1: 4 × 7 = 28. Step 2: 28 + 10 = 38. Answer: 38 stickers. | 1 point |
| Correct multiplication but arithmetic error in addition | 0.5 points |
| Correct answer with no work shown | 0.5 points |

## Rubric and Mastery Determination

| Level | Score Range | Meaning | Next Steps |
|-------|-----------|---------|------------|
| **Mastered** | 18-20 (90-100%) with at least 50% in each section | Fluent with all single-digit multiplication facts; can apply strategies and solve problems | Advance to Unit 3 (Division as the Inverse of Multiplication). Continue daily fact practice for maintenance. |
| **Approaching** | 14-17 (70-89%) | Good progress but gaps remain in specific areas | Identify weak sections. If fluency (A): targeted fact practice with games. If strategy (B): reteach specific strategies. If word problems (D): practice the three-question framework. Reassess weak sections after reteaching. |
| **Developing** | Below 14 (<70%) | Significant gaps in fact fluency or strategy understanding | Determine which fact families are weakest. Reteach those lessons (1-6) with emphasis on the strategy. Do NOT advance to division until multiplication facts are at least approaching mastery. |

### Section-Specific Reteaching Guide

| If Struggling In... | Focus On... |
|---|---|
| Section A (Fact Fluency) | Identify the specific facts missed. Practice those fact families using the games from Lesson 9. Make personal flash cards with the strategy on the back. Aim for 5-10 minutes of daily practice. |
| Section B (Strategy Knowledge) | Reteach the specific strategy that was weak. The student may recall the fact but not the strategy — this matters because strategies are the safety net for forgotten facts. Practice "show your strategy" problems. |
| Section C (Square Numbers) | Return to Lesson 7. Build square arrays on grid paper. The visual connection is essential. Focus on memorizing the tricky squares: 7 × 7 = 49, 8 × 8 = 64. |
| Section D (Word Problems) | Return to Lesson 10. Practice the three-question framework. For two-step problems, use a structured template: "Step 1: ___. Step 2: ___." Read problems aloud and have the student retell them in their own words before solving. |
| Section E (Cumulative Review) | If Unit 1 skills have faded, interleave ×2, ×5, and ×10 problems into daily practice. Briefly review the meaning of multiplication as equal groups. |

---

## Facilitator Observations

Record during the assessment:

- Does the student complete Section A within 4 minutes? If not, which facts took the longest?
- When explaining strategies (Section B), does the student describe the steps confidently, or are they guessing?
- Does the student read word problems carefully, or rush and miss details (especially in two-step problems)?
- Does the student show work, or rely entirely on mental math? (Both are acceptable, but showing work helps diagnose errors.)
- Overall confidence level: does the student seem comfortable with multiplication, or anxious?

---

## Post-Assessment Actions

**If the student achieves mastery (90%+):**
1. Celebrate the accomplishment! Completing all single-digit multiplication facts is a major milestone.
2. Begin Unit 3: Division as the Inverse of Multiplication.
3. Continue brief daily fact practice (5 minutes) to maintain fluency. Use games from Lesson 9.

**If the student does not achieve mastery:**
1. Do NOT rush to the next unit. Multiplication fact fluency is the foundation for division.
2. Reteach the weakest areas (see the section-specific guide above).
3. Reassess after 1-2 weeks of targeted practice.
4. Consider a shorter reassessment that focuses only on the weak sections rather than the full 20-item assessment.
