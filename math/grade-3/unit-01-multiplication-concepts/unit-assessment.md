# Unit Assessment — Grade 3 Math, Unit 1: Understanding Multiplication

**Grade:** 3
**Unit:** 1 — Understanding Multiplication
**Assessment Type:** Summative (unit-level)
**Administration:** Written; facilitator reads word problems aloud
**Estimated Duration:** 25-30 minutes
**Materials:** Paper and pencil. Counters available on request (note if used).

---

## Assessment Overview

| Section | Skill Assessed | Lesson Source | Items |
|---------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| A | Conceptual understanding (equal groups, arrays, vocabulary) | Lessons 1-4 | 4 |
| B | Properties (commutative, identity, zero) | Lessons 5-6 | 3 |
| C | Fact fluency (×2, ×5, ×10) | Lessons 7-9 | 6 |
| D | Word problems | Lesson 10 | 3 |
| — | **Total** | — | **16** |

**Mastery threshold:** 80% (13 of 16 correct) AND at least 50% in each section.

---

## Administration Instructions

Students write answers and show work. Read Section D word problems aloud. No time limit.

---

## Section A: Conceptual Understanding (4 items)

**A1.** Draw a picture showing 3 groups of 4. How many altogether?

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Drawing shows 3 equal groups with 4 objects each. Total: 12. | 1 point |

**A2.** Write the repeated addition AND the multiplication for this array:
```
● ● ● ● ●
● ● ● ● ●
● ● ● ● ●
```

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Repeated addition: 5 + 5 + 5 = 15. Multiplication: 3 × 5 = 15. | 1 point |
| Only one form correct | 0.5 points |

**A3.** How many rows and columns are in this array? Write a multiplication equation.
```
● ● ● ●
● ● ● ●
```

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| 2 rows, 4 columns. 2 × 4 = 8. | 1 point |

**A4.** "What does 4 × 6 mean? Explain in words."

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| "4 groups of 6" or "4 times 6" with clear reference to groups/repeated addition | 1 point |
| "24" (correct answer but no explanation of meaning) | 0.5 points |

## Rubric and Mastery Determination

| Level | Score Range | Meaning | Next Steps |
|-------|-----------|---------|------------|
| **Mastered** | 13-16 (80-100%) with ≥50% per section | Solid understanding of multiplication concepts and fluency with ×2, ×5, ×10 | Advance to Unit 2 (Multiplication Facts: 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9). |
| **Approaching** | 10-12 (63-79%) | Partial understanding; specific gaps | Reteach weak sections. If conceptual (A), revisit Lessons 1-4 with manipulatives. If fluency (C), practice skip-counting and doubles. |
| **Developing** | Below 10 (<63%) | Significant gaps in multiplication understanding | Determine if the issue is conceptual (doesn't understand groups) or prerequisite (weak addition). Address root cause before advancing. |

### Section-Specific Reteaching Guide

| If struggling in... | Focus on... |
|---|---|
| Section A (Conceptual) | Return to physical manipulatives. Build groups, draw arrays. The student needs more concrete experience before symbols. |
| Section B (Properties) | Review Lessons 5-6. The commutative property needs arrays; identity/zero need cups-and-counters demonstration. |
| Section C (Fact Fluency) | Identify which fact family is weak (2s, 5s, or 10s). Practice skip-counting for that family. Use flash cards. |
| Section D (Word Problems) | If computation is right but equation is wrong: practice "How many groups? How many in each?" If computation is wrong: fix facts first. |

---

## Facilitator Observations

- Does the student solve ×2, ×5, ×10 facts quickly (within 5 seconds) or slowly?
- Does the student use skip-counting, recall from memory, or repeated addition?
- Can the student explain multiplication in their own words?
- Does the student use drawings or models for word problems?
