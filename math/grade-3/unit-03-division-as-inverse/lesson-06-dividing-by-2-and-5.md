# Math — Grade 3 — Unit 3, Lesson 6

## Topic
Dividing by 2 and 5

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this lesson, the student will be able to:
- Solve ÷2 problems fluently (using halving or "think multiplication")
- Solve ÷5 problems fluently (using ×5 facts or skip counting backward)
- Recognize that dividing by 2 means finding half

## Prerequisites
- Fact families (Lesson 5)
- ×2 and ×5 fluency (Units 1-2)

## Materials Needed
- Paper and pencil
- Multiplication chart (for reference)

---

## Warm-Up (5 minutes)
**Purpose:** Activate ×2 and ×5 facts.

1. "2 × 7 = ?" (14) "5 × 7 = ?" (35)
2. "14 ÷ 2 = ?" (7) "35 ÷ 5 = ?" (7)
3. "Dividing by 2 and 5 is easy when you know your ×2 and ×5 facts!"

---

## Direct Instruction (10 minutes)
**Purpose:** Build ÷2 and ÷5 fluency through patterns.

### Instruction Notes

**Dividing by 2 = Halving.**
"Dividing by 2 means splitting into 2 equal groups. That's the same as finding half."
- 8 ÷ 2 = 4 (half of 8 is 4)
- 12 ÷ 2 = 6 (half of 12 is 6)
- 16 ÷ 2 = 8 (half of 16 is 8)
- 20 ÷ 2 = 10 (half of 20 is 10)

"Think multiplication: ? × 2 = 16. Answer: 8."

| Dividend | ÷ 2 | Think |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 1 | ? × 2 = 2 |
| 4 | 2 | ? × 2 = 4 |
| 6 | 3 | ? × 2 = 6 |

| 18 | 9 | ? × 2 = 18 |
| 20 | 10 | ? × 2 = 20 |

**Dividing by 5.**
"Use your ×5 facts backward."
- 15 ÷ 5 = 3 (because 3 × 5 = 15)
- 30 ÷ 5 = 6 (because 6 × 5 = 30)
- 45 ÷ 5 = 9 (because 9 × 5 = 45)

## Independent Practice (10 minutes)
**Purpose:** Build ÷2 and ÷5 fluency.

**★ On-level:**
1. 8 ÷ 2 = ___ (4)
2. 14 ÷ 2 = ___ (7)
3. 20 ÷ 2 = ___ (10)
4. 20 ÷ 5 = ___ (4)
5. 35 ÷ 5 = ___ (7)
6. 50 ÷ 5 = ___ (10)

**★★ Stretch:**
7. Fill in: ___ ÷ 2 = 8 (16)
8. Fill in: ___ ÷ 5 = 7 (35)
9. "There are 16 shoes. Shoes come in pairs (groups of 2). How many pairs?" (8)
10. "There are 45 fingers in the room. Each person has 5 fingers on one hand. How many hands are showing?" (9)

**★★★ Challenge:**
11. "What is half of 100?" (50) "Write it as a division sentence." (100 ÷ 2 = 50)
12. "100 ÷ 5 = ?" (20) "How do you know?" (Because 20 × 5 = 100, or count by 5s twenty times to reach 100.)

### Interleaved Review
- "Write the fact family for 3, 9, 27." (3×9=27, 9×3=27, 27÷3=9, 27÷9=3) *(Lesson 5)*
- "6 × 8 = ___" (48)

## Check for Understanding (5 minutes)
**Purpose:** Confirm ÷2 and ÷5 fluency.

**Exit Ticket:**

1. 16 ÷ 2 = ___ (8)
2. 40 ÷ 5 = ___ (8)

**Mastery = Both correct within a few seconds.**

### If Mastery Is Not Met
- ÷2: Practice halving with objects. "Split these 14 cubes into 2 equal groups."
- ÷5: Practice ×5 facts until they're automatic, then derive division.

### If Mastery Is Met
Move to Lesson 7 (Dividing by 3 and 4).

---

## Teacher/Facilitator Notes

**÷2 and ÷5 are the easiest division facts** because the corresponding multiplication facts are typically the most fluent. Start here to build confidence.

**"Halving" is a lifelong skill.** Dividing by 2 appears everywhere: splitting a recipe, sharing evenly, finding midpoints. Emphasize this real-world connection.
