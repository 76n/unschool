### Part B — Short Answer

**11.**
a) A = 8 × 5 = **40 sq cm**
b) P = 8+5+8+5 = **26 cm**

**12.**
- Top: 2 × 4 = 8 sq cm
- Bottom: 3 × 6 = 18 sq cm
- Total: 8 + 18 = **26 sq cm**

**13.**
- 6 + length + 6 + length = 32
- 12 + 2×length = 32
- 2×length = 20
- Length = **10 m**

**14.**
- 2:18 + 35 min = 2:53 P.M.
- Clock hands: minute hand on the 10 (and 3 more minutes = between the 10 and 11); hour hand just past the 2
- New time = **2:53 P.M.**

**15.**
- 2 kg = 2,000 g
- 2,000 + 750 = **2,750 g**

**16.**
- Bars: Red = 3 squares high (15÷5=3); Blue = 5 squares (25÷5=5); Green = 2 squares (10÷5=2); Yellow = 4 squares (20÷5=4)
- Title: any reasonable title such as "Favorite Color Survey"
- All bars drawn and labeled correctly — **1 point for correct graph**

**17.**
- Blue: 25; Green: 10; 25 − 10 = **15 more students**

**18.**
- 500 − 200 = 300 mL (after drinking)
- 300 + 150 = **450 mL** (after refilling)

**19.**
- 3:15 → 4:00 = 45 min
- 4:00 → 5:00 = 60 min
- Total: 45 + 60 = **1 hour 45 minutes**

**20.**
- P = 24; square has 4 equal sides; 24 ÷ 4 = 6 ft per side
- A = 6 × 6 = **36 sq ft**

### Part C — Extended Response

**21.**
a) A = 10 × 6 = **60 sq m**
b) P = 10+6+10+6 = **32 m**
c) 32 × $4 = **$128**

**22.**
a) Bella: 7 × 3 = **21 books**
b) Total symbols: 4+7+2+5 = 18; 18 × 3 = **54 books total**
c) Alex+Carlos: (4+2)×3 = 18 books; Bella+Diana: (7+5)×3 = 36 books; Difference: 36−18 = **18 more books**

**23.**
a) 10:45 + 1 hr 25 min: 10:45→11:00 = 15 min; 11:00+1 hr = 12:00; 12:00+10 min = 12:10 P.M. → Wait: 15+60+10 = 85 min = 1 hr 25 min ✓; Closes at **12:10 P.M.**
b) 10:45 − 30 min: 10:45→10:15; Food stands opened at **10:15 A.M.**
c) 10:45 A.M. to 3:30 P.M.: 10:45→11:00=15 min; 11:00→3:00=4 hr; 3:00→3:30=30 min; Total = 4 hr 45 min = **4 hours 45 minutes**

**24.**
a) P = 9+4+9+4 = **26 feet** of ribbon
b) A = 9 × 4 = **36 sheets** of paper
c) Ribbon: 26 × $2 = $52; Paper: 36 × $1 = $36; Total = $52 + $36 = **$88**

**25.**
a) 350+420+1,000+280+850 = **2,900 g**
b) 2,900 + 200 = **3,100 g** (= 3 kg 100 g)
c) 2 L = 2,000 mL; 2,000 − 750 = **1,250 mL** more
