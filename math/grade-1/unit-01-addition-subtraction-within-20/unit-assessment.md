# Unit Assessment — Grade 1 Math, Unit 1: Addition and Subtraction Within 20

**Grade:** 1
**Unit:** 1 — Addition and Subtraction Within 20
**Assessment Type:** Summative (unit-level)
**Administration:** One-on-one or written (student can do paper-based items independently; word problems read aloud)
**Estimated Duration:** 25-30 minutes
**Materials:** Paper, pencil, counting objects and ten-frames (available for support, not required)

## Administration Instructions

The student may use objects or the ten-frame for any computation item. For word problems (Section E), read the problem aloud. Allow the student to work at their own pace. Do not provide strategy hints during the assessment.

## Rubric and Mastery Determination

| Level | Score Range | Meaning | Next Steps |
|-------|-----------|---------|------------|
| **Mastered** | 17-20 (85-100%) with ≥1 per section | Strong addition/subtraction within 20 | Advance to Unit 2: Place Value (Tens and Ones). Continue fact fluency practice. |
| **Approaching** | 14-16 (70-84%) | Most skills present; specific gaps | Reteach struggling sections. Extra practice sessions before advancing. |
| **Developing** | Below 14 (<70%) | Significant gaps in strategies or computation | Identify which strategies are missing. Return to those specific lessons. Do not advance until within-20 operations are reliable. |

### Section-Specific Reteaching Guide

| If struggling in... | Focus on... |
|---|---|
| Section A (Addition) | Identify which strategies are missing. Reteach Make 10 (Lesson 5) if the student still counts all. Practice doubles (Lesson 3) for quick wins. |
| Section B (Subtraction) | The think-addition approach (Lesson 7). If the student can add but not subtract, the connection hasn't been made. Return to fact families. |
| Section C (Fact Families) | Physical modeling: build 8+5=13, then take apart to show 13−5=8. Practice with doubles families first (simpler). |
| Section D (Equal Sign) | Balance scale activities. Write equations in all formats daily. This is a conceptual lesson that needs repeated exposure. |
| Section E (Word Problems) | Identify which problem type is hardest. Practice with physical acting-out. Draw bar models to organize information. |

## Strategy Observation Notes

During the assessment, note which strategies the student uses:

| Problem | Strategy Observed |
|---------|-------------------|
| A1 (9+3) | □ Count all □ Count on □ Make 10 □ Other |
| A2 (7+7) | □ Count all □ Doubles □ Other |
| A3 (8+6) | □ Count on □ Make 10 □ Near-doubles □ Other |
| A5 (9+8) | □ Make 10 □ Near-doubles □ Count on □ Other |
| B2 (13−8) | □ Count back □ Think addition □ Objects □ Other |
| B3 (17−9) | □ Count back □ Think addition □ Objects □ Other |

This helps inform instruction: a student who gets correct answers but still counts all from 1 needs strategy development even though their computation is accurate.
