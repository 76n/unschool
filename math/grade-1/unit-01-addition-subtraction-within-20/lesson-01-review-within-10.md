# Math — Grade 1 — Unit 1, Lesson 1

## Topic
Review: Addition and Subtraction Within 10

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this lesson, the student will be able to:
- Add within 10 fluently using objects, drawings, or mental strategies
- Subtract within 10 fluently using objects, drawings, or mental strategies
- Recall pairs that make 10 (10-complements)

## Prerequisites
- Kindergarten Units 1-2 mastery (counting, addition, subtraction within 10)

## Materials Needed
- 10 counting objects in two colors
- Ten-frame
- Number cards 0-10
- Paper and pencil

---

## Warm-Up (5 minutes)
**Purpose:** Assess baseline — how much does the student remember from Kindergarten?

1. "What is 3 + 4?" (7)
2. "What is 8 − 3?" (5)
3. "What two numbers make 10? Tell me as many as you can." (Student names pairs: 1+9, 2+8, 3+7, 4+6, 5+5...)
4. Count from 1 to 20. (Establishes oral counting range for this unit)

---

## Direct Instruction (10 minutes)
**Purpose:** Quick review and strategy consolidation for within-10 facts.

### Key Vocabulary
- **Fact:** A number sentence that is always true (e.g., 3 + 5 = 8)
- **Make 10:** Pairs of numbers that add up to 10 — the most important facts to know well

### Instruction Notes

**Step 1: Review addition strategies.**
"In kindergarten, you learned to add by putting groups together. Let's make sure that's solid."

Rapid-fire with objects available: 2+5, 4+3, 1+6, 5+5, 3+7.

If the student counts all from 1, that's acceptable but note it — Lesson 2 introduces counting-on as a faster strategy.

**Step 2: Review subtraction.**
"And you learned to subtract by taking away."

8−2, 7−4, 10−3, 6−6, 9−5. Student uses objects if needed.

**Step 3: Make 10 pairs — rapid review.**
Flash the ten-frame. Fill with various amounts and ask: "How many empty spaces?" The answer + the filled amount = 10.

- 7 filled → 3 empty (7 + 3 = 10)
- 4 filled → 6 empty (4 + 6 = 10)
- 9 filled → 1 empty (9 + 1 = 10)

"These pairs are your superpower for this unit. We'll use them a LOT."

### Worked Examples

**Example 1:** 6 + 3 = ? Place 6 on the ten-frame, add 3. Count: 9.

**Example 2:** 10 − 4 = ? Start with 10 on the full ten-frame. Remove 4. Count remaining: 6.

---

## Guided Practice (10 minutes)
**Purpose:** Build fluency and identify any gaps from Kindergarten.

**Activity 1: Addition round (4 minutes)**
Student solves orally (objects available if needed):
- 5 + 3 = ___ (8)
- 2 + 7 = ___ (9)
- 4 + 4 = ___ (8)
- 6 + 4 = ___ (10)
- 0 + 8 = ___ (8)

**Activity 2: Subtraction round (3 minutes)**
- 9 − 4 = ___ (5)
- 7 − 7 = ___ (0)
- 10 − 6 = ___ (4)
- 8 − 5 = ___ (3)

**Activity 3: Make 10 speed drill (3 minutes)**
Facilitator says a number 0-10. Student says what number makes 10.
"I say 3, you say ___" (7). "I say 8, you say ___" (2). Continue rapidly.

*Facilitator prompt: If the student needs the ten-frame for make-10 pairs, use it — but aim for the student to start answering without it by the end of this activity.*

---

## Independent Practice (10 minutes)
**Purpose:** Confirm fluency within 10.

**★ On-level:**
1. 4 + 5 = ___ (9)
2. 7 − 2 = ___ (5)
3. 3 + 6 = ___ (9)
4. 10 − 7 = ___ (3)
5. ___ + 4 = 10 (6)
6. 8 − ___ = 3 (5)

**★★ Stretch:**
7. Write all the pairs that make 10.
8. "Is 4 + 3 the same as 3 + 4? How do you know?" (Yes — commutative property)

**★★★ Challenge:**
9. "I'm thinking of two numbers that add to 9. One number is 2 more than the other. What are they?" (Hint: doubles-adjacent. Answer: 3.5 + 5.5 doesn't work for whole numbers, but 4 + 5 works if we relax "2 more" — actually, this should be: one number is 1 more than the other → 4 + 5. Let me fix: "One is 3 more than the other" → 3 + 6.)
10. Find three numbers that add to 10. (e.g., 2 + 3 + 5, 1 + 4 + 5, etc.)

### Interleaved Review
- "Count backward from 20 to 10." (Counting extension)
- "Write the number 17." (Numeral writing review)

---

## Check for Understanding (5 minutes)
**Purpose:** Confirm readiness for strategies within 20.

**Exit Ticket:**

1. 7 + 3 = ___ (10)
2. 9 − 5 = ___ (4)
3. ___ + 6 = 10 (4)

**Mastery = All 3 correct. If not, revisit Kindergarten materials before proceeding.**

### If Mastery Is Not Met
Return to Kindergarten Unit 2 for targeted reteaching. This is a prerequisite unit — proceeding without fluency within 10 will make everything harder.

### If Mastery Is Met
Move to Lesson 2 (Counting On to Add).

---

## Teacher/Facilitator Notes

**This lesson is diagnostic.** Its main purpose is to identify where the student stands. If addition and subtraction within 10 are solid, this lesson takes 20 minutes and you move on. If there are gaps, pause and address them.

**Make 10 pairs are critical.** The entire "Make 10" strategy (Lesson 5) depends on knowing these pairs fluently. If the student is shaky, practice make-10 pairs daily for a week before starting Lesson 5.
