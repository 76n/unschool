# Math — Grade 8 — Unit 3, Lesson 6

## Topic
Slope-Intercept Form

## Learning Objectives
- Identify the slope (m) and y-intercept (b) from an equation written in slope-intercept form y = mx + b.
- Graph a linear function using the slope and y-intercept.
- Write the equation of a line given a graph showing the slope and y-intercept.
- Interpret slope and y-intercept in real-world contexts.

## Prerequisites
- Understanding of slope and how to calculate it (Lesson 5)
- Understanding of linear functions (Lesson 4)
- Ability to plot points and draw lines on a coordinate plane (Grade 6-7)

## Materials Needed
- Whiteboard and markers (or projector).
- Graph paper or pre-printed coordinate planes.
- Ruler or straightedge.
- Colored pencils (for distinguishing multiple lines on the same graph).
- Student worksheet (Guided Practice and Independent Practice problems).
- Index cards or half-sheets for Exit Ticket.

**Standards:** 8.F.3 — Interpret the equation y = mx + b as defining a linear function, whose graph is a straight line; give examples of functions that are not linear. 8.F.4 — Construct a function to model a linear relationship between two quantities. Determine the rate of change and initial value of the function.

## Warm-Up (5 minutes)
**Purpose:** Review slope and connect it to an equation form.

1. Find the slope of the line through (0, 3) and (2, 9). (m = (9 - 3)/(2 - 0) = 6/2 = 3)
2. What is the y-value when x = 0? (y = 3)
3. Using the pattern "start at 3, add 3 each time," write an equation relating x and y. (y = 3x + 3)
4. Ask: "The equation y = 3x + 3 tells us two things: the slope is 3 and the starting value is 3. Today we will study this form in detail."

## Direct Instruction (10-15 minutes)

### Key Vocabulary
- **Slope-intercept form** — the equation y = mx + b, where m is the slope and b is the y-intercept.
- **y-intercept** — the point where the line crosses the y-axis; the value of y when x = 0. Written as the ordered pair (0, b).
- **m** — the slope of the line (rate of change).
- **b** — the y-intercept of the line (initial value).

### Instruction Notes

**Step 1 — Introduce slope-intercept form.**

Write on the board: y = mx + b.

Say: "This is called SLOPE-INTERCEPT FORM because it shows you both the slope and the y-intercept directly. The number multiplied by x is the slope, m. The number added at the end is the y-intercept, b."

Write examples and identify m and b:
- y = 2x + 5: m = 2, b = 5.
- y = -3x + 1: m = -3, b = 1.
- y = x - 4: m = 1, b = -4. (The coefficient of x is 1, even though it is not written.)
- y = -x: m = -1, b = 0. (No constant term means b = 0.)
- y = 7: m = 0, b = 7. (No x term means m = 0 — horizontal line.)

**Step 2 — Demonstrate graphing from slope-intercept form.**

Graph y = 2x + 3.

Say: "Step 1: Find b and plot the y-intercept. Here b = 3, so plot the point (0, 3) on the y-axis."

Say: "Step 2: Use the slope to find the next point. The slope is 2, which means rise 2, run 1. From (0, 3), go up 2 and right 1 to reach (1, 5). Plot that point."

Say: "Step 3: Use the slope again for a third point. From (1, 5), go up 2 and right 1 to reach (2, 7). Plot that point."

Say: "Step 4: Draw a straight line through all three points. Extend it with arrows in both directions."

Describe the result: "The line crosses the y-axis at (0, 3) and rises steeply from left to right, passing through (1, 5) and (2, 7)."

**Step 3 — Demonstrate with a negative slope.**

Graph y = -x + 4.

Say: "The y-intercept is 4, so plot (0, 4). The slope is -1, which means rise -1 (go down 1), run 1 (go right 1). From (0, 4), go down 1 and right 1 to (1, 3). Then from (1, 3) to (2, 2). Then from (2, 2) to (3, 1)."

Describe the result: "The line crosses the y-axis at (0, 4) and falls gently from left to right, crossing the x-axis at (4, 0)."

**Step 4 — Demonstrate reading an equation from a graph.**

Describe a graph: "A straight line crosses the y-axis at (0, -2) and passes through (3, 4)."

Say: "The y-intercept is -2, so b = -2. Now find the slope: m = (4 - (-2))/(3 - 0) = 6/3 = 2. The equation is y = 2x - 2."

**Step 5 — Interpret in real-world context.**

Say: "A plumber charges $50 for a house call plus $30 per hour. The total cost is C = 30h + 50."

- The slope (30) means the cost increases by $30 for each additional hour of work.
- The y-intercept (50) is the starting cost — the house call fee before any work begins.
- C(0) = 50: even if the plumber works 0 hours, you still pay $50 for showing up.

### Worked Examples

**Example A: Identify slope and y-intercept, then graph**
y = 3x - 2.

Slope: m = 3. Y-intercept: b = -2, so the line crosses the y-axis at (0, -2).

Graphing steps:
1. Plot (0, -2).
2. Slope = 3 = 3/1. From (0, -2), go up 3 and right 1 to (1, 1).
3. From (1, 1), go up 3 and right 1 to (2, 4).
4. Draw a line through (0, -2), (1, 1), and (2, 4).

The line rises steeply from left to right, crossing the y-axis below the origin.

**Example B: Identify slope and y-intercept — fractional slope**
y = (1/2)x + 1.

Slope: m = 1/2. Y-intercept: b = 1, so the line crosses the y-axis at (0, 1).

Graphing steps:
1. Plot (0, 1).
2. Slope = 1/2. From (0, 1), go up 1 and right 2 to (2, 2).
3. From (2, 2), go up 1 and right 2 to (4, 3).
4. Draw a line through (0, 1), (2, 2), and (4, 3).

The line rises gently from left to right with a shallow slope.

**Example C: Write equation from graph description**
A line crosses the y-axis at (0, 6) and passes through (4, -2).

Slope: m = (-2 - 6)/(4 - 0) = -8/4 = -2.
Y-intercept: b = 6.
Equation: y = -2x + 6.

Check: When x = 4, y = -2(4) + 6 = -8 + 6 = -2. Correct.

## Guided Practice (10 minutes)

Work through these problems together. The student attempts each problem first, then discusses.

1. Identify the slope and y-intercept of y = 5x - 7.
   - *Answer:* Slope: 5. Y-intercept: -7 (the point (0, -7)).

2. Identify the slope and y-intercept of y = -4x.
   - *Answer:* Slope: -4. Y-intercept: 0 (the point (0, 0) — the line passes through the origin).

3. Graph y = -2x + 6. List three points on the line.
   - *Answer:* Y-intercept at (0, 6). Slope = -2: from (0, 6) go down 2, right 1 to (1, 4). From (1, 4) go down 2, right 1 to (2, 2). Three points: (0, 6), (1, 4), (2, 2). The line falls from left to right.

4. A line crosses the y-axis at (0, 1) and goes through (2, 7). Write the equation.
   - *Answer:* m = (7 - 1)/(2 - 0) = 6/2 = 3. b = 1. Equation: y = 3x + 1.

5. A phone plan costs $35 per month with a $50 activation fee. Write the equation in slope-intercept form and state what m and b represent.
   - *Answer:* C = 35m + 50 (using m for months). Slope (35) = monthly cost. Y-intercept (50) = activation fee, the cost before any months of service.

**Facilitator tip:** When graphing, always have the student plot at least three points. Two points define a line, but the third serves as a check. If the third point does not fall on the line, there is a calculation error somewhere.

## Independent Practice (10-15 minutes)

### Part A: Identify Slope and Y-Intercept
For each equation, state the slope and y-intercept.

1. y = 4x + 3
2. y = -x + 8
3. y = (2/3)x - 5
4. y = -6x
5. y = 12
6. y = x

### Part B: Graphing from Equations
Graph each line. List three points you use.

7. y = x + 2
8. y = -3x + 9
9. y = (1/2)x - 1
10. y = -x - 3

### Part C: Word Problems

11. A taxi company charges $2.50 per mile plus a $4.00 flat fee. Write the cost equation in slope-intercept form. Graph it for 0 to 8 miles. What does the slope represent? What does the y-intercept represent?
12. A hot tub is being filled with water. It starts with 50 gallons and fills at 8 gallons per minute. Write the function in slope-intercept form. How many gallons will it have after 15 minutes? Describe what the graph would look like.

### Answer Key

**Part A**
1. Slope: 4. Y-intercept: 3 (the point (0, 3)).
2. Slope: -1. Y-intercept: 8 (the point (0, 8)).
3. Slope: 2/3. Y-intercept: -5 (the point (0, -5)).
4. Slope: -6. Y-intercept: 0 (the point (0, 0)).
5. Slope: 0. Y-intercept: 12 (the point (0, 12)). Horizontal line.
6. Slope: 1. Y-intercept: 0 (the point (0, 0)).

**Part B**
7. y = x + 2. Points: (0, 2), (1, 3), (2, 4). Line rises gently, crosses y-axis at 2.
8. y = -3x + 9. Points: (0, 9), (1, 6), (2, 3). Line falls steeply, crosses y-axis at 9, crosses x-axis at (3, 0).
9. y = (1/2)x - 1. Points: (0, -1), (2, 0), (4, 1). Line rises slowly, crosses y-axis at -1.
10. y = -x - 3. Points: (0, -3), (1, -4), (2, -5). Line falls, crosses y-axis at -3.

**Part C**
11. C(m) = 2.50m + 4.00 (where m = miles). Slope (2.50) represents the cost per mile. Y-intercept (4.00) represents the flat fee charged regardless of distance. Graph: starts at (0, 4) and rises to (8, 24).
12. W(t) = 8t + 50 (where t = minutes). After 15 minutes: W(15) = 8(15) + 50 = 120 + 50 = 170 gallons. The graph starts at (0, 50) and is a straight line rising steadily from left to right.

## Assessment Check

### Exit Ticket
**Problem 1:** Write the equation of a line with slope -3 and y-intercept 10.
**Problem 2:** Graph y = 2x - 4. List three points on the line.

**Target response:**
1. y = -3x + 10.
2. Points: (0, -4), (1, -2), (2, 0). The line rises from left to right, crossing the y-axis at (0, -4) and the x-axis at (2, 0).

## Extension

- **Parallel Lines Investigation:** Graph y = 2x + 1, y = 2x - 3, and y = 2x + 5 on the same coordinate plane. What do you notice? (All three lines are parallel — they have the same slope but different y-intercepts.) Will these lines ever intersect? (No — parallel lines with different y-intercepts never intersect.)
- **Real-World Equation Writing:** Choose a real-world scenario that involves a starting value and a constant rate (e.g., savings account, plant growth, distance traveled). Write the equation in slope-intercept form, explain what m and b represent in your context, and graph the function for a meaningful range of inputs.
