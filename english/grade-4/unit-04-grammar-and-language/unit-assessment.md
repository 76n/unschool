## Answer Key

### Section 1: Relative Pronouns and Relative Adverbs (L.4.1a)

**1.** (a) who
*Explanation: The veterinarian is the subject — she is doing the treating. Who is used for subjects.*

**2.** (c) whose
*Explanation: The science project belongs to the student. Whose shows possession.*

**3.** (b) where
*Explanation: The museum is a place. Where introduces clauses about places.*

**4.** (c) which
*Explanation: The Statue of Liberty is a specific thing, and the clause is nonrestrictive (bonus information). Which is used with commas for nonrestrictive clauses about things.*

**5.** (b) when
*Explanation: Autumn is a time/season. When introduces clauses about times.*

**6.** The coach whom we respect very much trained our team.
*Explanation: We respect him (object), so use whom. Accept also: "The coach who trained our team is someone we respect very much."*

**7.** The bakery where my grandmother works sells the best cinnamon rolls.
*Explanation: The bakery is a place, so use the relative adverb where.*

**8.** Relative adverb
*Explanation: "Where" tells about a place and introduces a clause. It does not replace a noun — it is an adverb.*

### Section 2: Progressive Verb Tenses (L.4.1b)

**9.** The children are flying kites in the field.
*Explanation: Present progressive = are + flying. "Fly" drops the y — wait, actually "fly" becomes "flying" (change y to y + ing = flying). Subject "the children" = they, so use "are."*

**10.** The painter was finishing the mural on the library wall.
*Explanation: Past progressive = was + finishing. Subject "the painter" = he/she (singular), so use "was." Drop the silent e in "finish" — actually "finish" does not end in silent e, just add -ing: finishing.*

**11.** The school band will be performing at the spring concert.
*Explanation: Future progressive = will be + performing.*

**12.** is building
*Explanation: Present progressive. Subject "my sister" = she, so use "is." Build + ing = building.*

**13.** were playing
*Explanation: Past progressive. Subject "we" uses "were." Play + ing = playing.*

**14.** will be flying
*Explanation: Future progressive. Will be + flying.*

**15.** Past progressive
*Explanation: "Were planting" = were + verb-ing = past progressive.*

**16.** Future progressive
*Explanation: "Will be transforming" = will be + verb-ing = future progressive.*

### Section 3: Ordering Adjectives (L.4.1d)

**17.** a delicious old Italian pasta
*Explanation: Opinion (delicious) → Age (old) → Origin (Italian).*

**18.** a tiny brown leather wallet
*Explanation: Size (tiny) → Color (brown) → Material (leather).*

**19.** She found a small round wooden box in the attic.
*Explanation: Size (small) → Shape (round) → Material (wooden).*

**20.** (b) He wore a silly big red hat to the costume party.
*Explanation: Opinion (silly) → Size (big) → Color (red).*

### Section 4: Precise Word Choice and Formal/Informal Language (L.4.3a)

**21.** Sample answers (accept any precise alternative):
- The kitten scampered across the kitchen floor.
- The kitten crept across the kitchen floor.
- The kitten darted across the kitchen floor.
*Explanation: "Went" is vague. Precise verbs tell how the kitten moved.*

**22.** Sample answers:
- The thunderstorm was terrifying, with crashing thunder and blinding rain.
- The thunderstorm was fierce and destructive.
*Explanation: "Bad" is vague. Precise words describe what made the storm bad.*

**23.** Sample answers:
- "I want to go to the park," the boy pleaded.
- "I want to go to the park," the boy whined.
- "I want to go to the park," the boy begged.
*Explanation: "Said" is vague. Precise verbs show how the boy spoke.*

**24.** Formal
*Explanation: Complete sentence, no contractions, polished vocabulary ("express my appreciation," "opportunity to participate").*

**25.** Informal
*Explanation: Uses "Hey," "awesome," "totally," and a casual, conversational tone.*

**26.** Sample answers:
- The food served in the cafeteria today was unappetizing, and most students did not enjoy it.
- Today's cafeteria lunch was not enjoyable, and the majority of students were dissatisfied.
*Explanation: Remove "super gross" (slang), "nobody" (informal), and make the tone professional. Avoid contractions.*

### Section 5: Capitalization (L.4.2a)

**27.** My family visited the Grand Canyon in Arizona last August.
*Explanation: "My" starts the sentence. "Grand Canyon" is a geographical name. "Arizona" is a state. "August" is a month.*

**28.** Doctor Chen, who works at Mercy General Hospital, is our family doctor.
*Explanation: "Doctor Chen" — title before a name. "Mercy General Hospital" — specific organization. "doctor" at the end is a common noun used alone, so it stays lowercase.*

**29.** We are reading the book Charlotte's Web by E.B. White in our English class.
*Explanation: "We" starts the sentence. "Charlotte's Web" is a book title. "E.B. White" is a person's name. "English" is a proper adjective (derived from a proper noun).*

**30.** Last summer, our team the Riverside Rockets won the State Championship at Jefferson Stadium.
*Explanation: "Summer" should NOT be capitalized (seasons are not capitalized). "Riverside Rockets" is a team name (capitalize). "State Championship" is the name of a specific event (capitalize). "Jefferson Stadium" is a specific place (capitalize).*

### Reteaching Recommendations
