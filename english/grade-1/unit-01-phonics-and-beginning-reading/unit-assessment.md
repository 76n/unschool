# Unit Assessment — Grade 1 ELA, Unit 1: Phonics and Beginning Reading

**Grade:** 1
**Unit:** 1 — Phonics and Beginning Reading
**Assessment Type:** Summative (unit-level)
**Administration:** One-on-one (facilitator reads instructions; student reads words, passages, and writes responses)
**Estimated Duration:** 30-35 minutes
**Materials:** This assessment, paper, pencil. No word cards, letter tiles, or other aids — this assesses independent skills.

---

## Administration Instructions

This assessment is administered one-on-one. The facilitator presents each section and the student responds orally (for reading items) or in writing (for spelling items). For passage reading (Section G), the student reads aloud while the facilitator tracks accuracy.

**Important notes:**
- Do NOT provide phonics hints during the assessment (e.g., do not say "look for the vowel team" or "remember the silent e rule").
- DO encourage the student: "Take your time. You can do this."
- If the student gets stuck on a word for more than 10 seconds, say "Let's move to the next one" and mark it as incorrect.
- Record observations about the student's strategies (sounding out, guessing, self-correcting) in the observation notes at the end.

## Section F: Nonsense Word Reading (6 points)

**Purpose:** This section tests whether the student can truly DECODE using phonics patterns, or whether they have simply memorized the words from the lessons. Nonsense words cannot be memorized — they must be sounded out.

**Instructions to student:** "These are made-up words — they are not real words. But they follow the same patterns you've learned. Read each one using your phonics skills. It's okay if they sound funny!"

**F1.** feg (CVC, short e)

**F2.** chum (digraph ch + short u)

**F3.** blid (beginning blend bl + short i)

**F4.** dake (CVCe, long a)

**F5.** foat (vowel team oa, long o)

**F6.** migh (igh pattern, long i — rhymes with "high")

| Item | Expected Pronunciation | Score |
|------|----------------------|-------|
| F1 | /f/ /e/ /g/ — "feg" | ___ / 1 |
| F2 | /ch/ /u/ /m/ — "chum" (note: chum IS a real word, but it is obscure enough to function as a decoding test for this age) | ___ / 1 |
| F3 | /bl/ /i/ /d/ — "blid" | ___ / 1 |
| F4 | /d/ /ay/ /k/ — "dake" (long a, silent e) | ___ / 1 |
| F5 | /f/ /oh/ /t/ — "foat" (oa vowel team) | ___ / 1 |
| F6 | /m/ /iy/ — "migh" (rhymes with "high") | ___ / 1 |

**Facilitator note:** If the student reads a nonsense word using correct phonics but with a slightly different pronunciation than expected (e.g., pronounces "feg" with a short i instead of short e), note this but give credit if the consonant sounds and blending are correct. The key question is: can the student apply the patterns, or are they guessing?

**Instructions to student:** "Now you are going to read a short story aloud. Take your time and use all the phonics patterns you know. After you read, I'll ask you some questions about the story."

### Passage: "The Goat and the Toad"

> A goat was on a road. The road was long and the sun was bright. The goat met a toad. "What a fine day!" said the goat. "Yes," said the toad. "But I need shade. The sun is too much." The goat and the toad went to a big oak tree. It had thick green leaves. "This is the best spot," said the toad. They both sat and rested. When the sun went down, the toad said, "Thank you, goat. You are a good friend."

## Rubric and Mastery Determination

| Level | Score Range | Meaning | Next Steps |
|-------|-----------|---------|------------|
| **Mastered** | 34-40 (85-100%) with at least 50% per section | Strong phonics and beginning reading skills | Advance to Unit 2. Continue daily reading practice. Include phonics review in warm-ups. |
| **Approaching** | 28-33 (70-84%) | Most phonics patterns are present; specific gaps remain | Identify weak sections. Reteach those specific patterns (1-2 lessons). Reassess before advancing. |
| **Developing** | Below 28 (<70%) | Significant phonics gaps | Identify which patterns are missing. Return to those lessons systematically. Do not advance until foundational patterns are solid. |

---

## Section-Specific Reteaching Guide

| If struggling in... | Focus on... |
|---|---|
| Section A (CVC words) | This is the foundation. Return to Lesson 1. Practice blending three sounds. Use word families (cat, hat, mat; pig, big, dig). The student cannot succeed in later patterns without this base. |
| Section B (Digraphs) | Determine which digraphs are problematic. Practice one at a time. Use the "two letters, one sound" concept with physical cards taped together. Return to Lessons 2-3. |
| Section C (Blends) | Check whether the student adds vowels between blend letters ("buh-lack" for "black"). Practice sliding the two sounds together without inserting a vowel. Focus on l-blends before r-blends. Return to Lessons 4-5. |
| Section D (CVCe) | The silent e rule may not be internalized. Use the "magic e" transformation activity (cap → cape) extensively. Practice with one vowel at a time. Return to Lesson 6. |
| Section E (Vowel teams) | Determine which teams are weakest. Practice one pair at a time (ai/ay, then ee/ea, then oa/ow, then igh). Use word families and sorting. Return to Lessons 7-8. |
| Section F (Nonsense words) | Low scores here suggest the student is memorizing rather than decoding. Return to CVC blending practice. Ensure the student can sound out unfamiliar words, not just recognize familiar ones. This is a critical skill. |
| Section G (Passage reading) | If accuracy is low: return to word-level practice for the patterns that caused errors. If fluency is low: practice re-reading familiar passages. If comprehension is low: practice reading short sentences and asking "what happened?" after each one. Return to Lessons 9-10. |

## Cumulative Review Items (Kindergarten Skills)

These items are not scored as part of the unit assessment but provide valuable diagnostic information about foundational skills:

**CR1.** "Say the alphabet." (Student recites A-Z)
- [ ] Complete and accurate  [ ] Minor errors  [ ] Significant gaps

**CR2.** "What sound does the letter M make?" (/m/) "What about the letter D?" (/d/) "And the letter S?" (/s/)
- [ ] All 3 correct  [ ] 1-2 correct  [ ] 0 correct

**CR3.** "Can you clap the sounds in the word 'cat'?" (3 claps: /k/ /a/ /t/)
- [ ] Correct segmentation  [ ] Incorrect

**Facilitator note:** If the student struggles with these cumulative items, the phonics patterns in this unit may be built on a shaky foundation. Consider revisiting kindergarten phonics before advancing to Unit 2.
